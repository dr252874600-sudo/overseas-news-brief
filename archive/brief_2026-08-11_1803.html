After appearing to board Air Force One for cameras, Trump was reportedly transported via a catering truck to a smaller C-32A military aircraft. Journalists and some White House staff on board the original plane were unaware of the switch. U.S. officials cited a credible threat of Iran firing a missile at the plane. The White House has not confirmed the maneuver. This clandestine operation occurred after the breakdown of U.S.-Iran negotiations and renewed U.S. military strikes against Iran. Trump had previously stated he was Iran's "number one target" during the summit. The exact details of how Trump returned to the legacy Air Force One for his onward journey remain unclear.

BBC、The New York Times · 2026-08-11 17:42
详细中文报道

叙利亚一家法院在缺席审判中判处被推翻的前总统巴沙尔·阿萨德死刑，罪名包括在叙利亚内战期间犯下的战争罪和反人类罪。阿萨德及其家人在2024年12月政权被推翻后逃往莫斯科，结束了其家族长达半个世纪的统治。在同一案件中，阿萨德的堂兄、前叙利亚南部德拉省政治安全主管阿特夫·纳吉布也被判处死刑。纳吉布在法庭上被法官宣读判决时出现在笼子里。德拉省被广泛认为是反抗阿萨德统治的起义发源地。2011年3月，一群被指控书写反政府涂鸦的青少年被拘留和据称遭受酷刑，引发了该市的抗议活动，叙利亚安全部队对此进行了暴力镇压。这一回应助长了全国范围内的示威活动，并最终升级为内战，造成数十万人死亡。叙利亚临时政府今年早些时候开始审判与阿萨德政权有关的官员，该政权统治该国超过50年。纳吉布是受审的最高级别官员之一。他被判犯有包括谋杀、“故意杀害15岁以下儿童”和“导致死亡的酷刑”在内的罪行。目前尚不清楚叙利亚新当局是否能够从莫斯科引渡阿萨德。这并非叙利亚新领导层首次针对阿萨德采取行动。去年12月，曾有人对他发出谋杀和酷刑的逮捕令。在艾哈迈德·沙拉（Ahmed al-Sharaa）领导的军队以11天的闪电战推翻阿萨德王朝后，他逃往俄罗斯。自那时以来，新政府拘留了许多前官员和安全人员，但包括阿萨德在内的绝大多数前政权高级人物仍身在俄罗斯，难以触及。在阿萨德统治的最后几年，叙利亚反对派人士试图通过西方国家的法院追究其责任，但均未获成功。 与此同时，在叙利亚南部由德鲁兹少数民族主导的斯维达省，许多居民表示，中央政府最终会介入并控制该地区只是时间问题。该省在内战期间相对独立，但随着其他地区被政府收复，其未来面临不确定性。德鲁兹社区对中央政府的信任度较低，并对可能发生的军事干预表示担忧。

Detailed English brief

A Syrian court has sentenced ousted dictator Bashar al-Assad to death in absentia, convicting him of war crimes and crimes against humanity during the country's civil war. His cousin, Atef Najib, the former head of political security in southern Syria's Deraa province, was also sentenced to death. Deraa is considered the birthplace of the uprising against Assad's rule. The verdict comes as many in Sweida Province, dominated by the country's Druse minority, fear a takeover by the central government. The province has maintained a degree of autonomy during the conflict, but its future is uncertain as other regions fall under government control. Many former regime officials, including Assad, have fled to Russia, making extradition complex. Attempts to prosecute Assad through Western courts during his rule were unsuccessful.

关注点 The death sentence against Bashar al-Assad, though in absentia, represents a significant legal and symbolic move against the former Syrian dictator. It highlights the ongoing pursuit of accountability for war crimes and crimes against humanity. The situation in Sweida Province also points to the fragmented nature of Syria and the potential for future instability as the central government seeks to consolidate power.

科技巨头斥资5000亿美元投资人工智能基础设施

Wall Street giants hand Nvidia $500bn to fund boom in AI projects
BBC、Intellectia AI、The Washington Post · 2026-08-11 16:44
详细中文报道

英伟达（Nvidia）公司宣布已与包括阿波罗（Apollo）、贝莱德（BlackRock）、黑石（Blackstone）、布鲁克菲尔德（Brookfield）、高盛（Goldman Sachs）和KKR在内的多家华尔街顶级金融机构达成协议，共同筹集高达5000亿美元的资金，专门用于投资人工智能（AI）基础设施。 此次融资标志着AI硬件和基础设施（通常被称为“算力”）首次被视为一种独立的资产类别，得到了全球主要长期资本提供者的支持。英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋表示，“在AI领域，算力即收入。我们正在汇集全球领先的长期资本提供者，独立承保AI基础设施。” 这笔巨额资金将用于支持英伟达自身的AI项目，以及由其合作伙伴正在建设的项目。具体而言，资金将投入到建设新的数据中心，以容纳、运营和冷却处理AI数据和任务的海量堆叠式计算机芯片。此外，资金还将用于建设新的工厂，以生产驱动这些AI系统所需的芯片，并提高其市场供应量。 KKR的联席首席执行官乔·裴（Joe Bae）和斯科特·纳塔尔（Scott Nuttall）在一份联合声明中指出，“算力已成为关键的基础设施资产。随着我们扩展数字基础设施的方法，我们了解到交付而非雄心是困难的部分。” 目前，几乎所有主要的科技和AI公司都在使用英伟达的图形处理单元（GPU）来驱动其服务、AI平台和AI聊天机器人。据报道，包括谷歌（Google）、Meta、亚马逊（Amazon）、微软（Microsoft）、SpaceX、特斯拉（Tesla）、OpenAI和Anthropic在内的公司，在过去三年中已在AI项目和基础设施上花费了超过1万亿美元，并且预计未来支出还将继续增加。对英伟达芯片的强劲需求是推动此次大规模融资的主要原因。 然而，一些市场分析师也表达了对这笔巨额投资的担忧。例如，Rathbones的高级投资经理简·赛登汉姆（Jane Sydenham）表示，“英伟达规模巨大，生产着每个人在AI领域都需要的芯片，并且需要继续促进AI的增长。但令人担忧的是，越来越多的资金投入到这些项目中，它们是否都能在未来获得应有的回报？” 这一疑问预示着AI投资的潜在风险和市场泡沫的可能性。

Detailed English brief

Nvidia has announced a landmark deal with major Wall Street firms, including Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR, to raise $500 billion for artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure. This significant capital infusion marks the first time AI hardware and "compute" are being treated as a distinct asset class by major long-term investors. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stated, "In AI, compute is revenue. We are bringing the world's leading long-term capital providers together to independently underwrite AI infrastructure." The funds will be allocated to Nvidia's own AI initiatives and those of its partners. Key investments will focus on building new data centers to house and operate the vast number of stacked computer chips required for AI processing, as well as constructing new factories to manufacture these essential AI chips and increase their availability. Companies like Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, SpaceX, Tesla, OpenAI, and Anthropic are heavily reliant on Nvidia's GPUs for their AI services. These tech giants have collectively spent over $1 trillion on AI projects and infrastructure in the last three years, with further substantial spending anticipated. The immense demand for Nvidia's chips is the primary driver behind this massive financing effort. Despite the surge in investment, some analysts express caution. Jane Sydenham, a senior investment manager at Rathbones, noted, "The worry is that more and more money is going into these projects. Are they all going to earn the right return for the future?" This sentiment highlights potential risks associated with the rapid expansion and high valuations in the AI sector.

关注点 This massive investment signals a significant acceleration in the AI race, with major financial institutions now viewing AI infrastructure as a core asset class. It underscores the critical role of Nvidia's hardware and the immense capital required to build out the foundational elements of AI. The scale of the funding could reshape the tech landscape and create new opportunities and risks for investors and companies alike.
