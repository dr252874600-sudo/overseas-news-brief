Guinea-Bissau referendum approves new constitution that expands president's powers

详细中文报道

几内亚比绍的选民已批准一项新宪法，该宪法大幅扩张了总统的权力，为今年晚些时候的议会选举做准备。这项改革由去年11月发动政变的军政府提出。根据新宪法，总统将有权任命和罢免总理及内阁成员，设立或撤销部委，并在发生严重政治危机时解散议会。此次公投的初步结果显示，70%的选民支持这些改革，30%的选民反对，投票率接近60%。主要反对党抵制了此次公投，认为该过程缺乏足够的政治和制度保障，并可能威胁国家重返民主的过渡。几内亚比绍是世界上最贫穷的国家之一，自1974年独立以来，至少经历过九次未遂或成功的政变。军政府辩称，这些改革是结束总统与总理之间反复争端所必需的。新宪法还规定，总统任期仍为五年，可连任两届。此外，议会席位将从102个减少到65个，选区数量从29个减少到12个。此举改变了该国半总统制下的权力平衡，加强了总统的权威，削弱了总理和议会的权力。联合国曾将该国称为“毒品国家”，其漫长的海岸线为毒品贩运提供了便利。

Detailed English brief

Voters in Guinea-Bissau have approved a new constitution in a referendum, significantly expanding presidential powers. The reforms, proposed by the military government that seized power last November, allow the president to appoint and dismiss the prime minister and cabinet, create or abolish ministries, and dissolve parliament in the event of a grave political crisis. Preliminary results indicate 70% of voters supported the changes, with a turnout of nearly 60%. The main opposition party boycotted the vote, citing a lack of adequate political and institutional safeguards and concerns about the country's transition back to democracy. Guinea-Bissau, one of the world's poorest nations, has a history of political instability, including at least nine attempted or successful coups since independence. The military government argues the changes are necessary to end recurring disputes between the president and prime minister. The new constitution retains the five-year presidential term limit but reduces the number of parliamentary seats from 102 to 65 and constituencies from 29 to 12, shifting the balance of power in the semi-presidential system.

关注点 The approval of a new constitution that significantly centralizes power in the presidency marks a critical juncture for Guinea-Bissau's political future. It raises concerns about democratic backsliding and the potential for further instability, especially given the country's history of coups and the opposition's boycott of the referendum. The changes could impact governance, political accountability, and the nation's ongoing efforts to establish stable civilian rule.

希腊与以色列于周一签署了一项价值35亿美元的防务协议，共同建造一个名为“阿喀琉斯之盾”（Achilles Shield）的综合、多层次防御网络，以保护希腊领土。该协议涵盖了以色列的“大卫弹弓”（David’s Sling）和“Spyder”拦截系统、巴拉克（Barak）防空和导弹防御系统以及多任务雷达。此外，双方还达成一项单独协议，为希腊战略要地增加“无人机穹顶”（Drone Dome）系统，以防御无人机威胁。这是两国之间有史以来最大的防务协议，也是以色列近期最重要的出口协议之一。尽管以色列的国际形象处于低谷，且希腊民众对其负面看法占多数，但雅典选择深化与以色列在防务和军事技术领域的合作。分析认为，这主要有两大原因。首先，以色列与伊朗的冲突阴影笼罩。今年3月，伊朗无人机袭击了希腊的邻国塞浦路斯，加剧了地区紧张局势。在以色列和美国成功拦截伊朗约300枚无人机和导弹后，希腊国防部长呼吁雅典开发自己的反无人机能力。因此，该协议部分是各国为应对日益增长的无人机威胁而寻求军事解决方案的务实需求。其次，该协议也符合以色列长期奉行的“周边战略”，即与黎凡特周边地区的非阿拉伯国家发展关系，以对抗其直接邻国的敌意。以色列拥有先进的防空和反无人机技术，而希腊则希望加强其国防能力，以应对地区安全挑战，特别是来自无人机和导弹的威胁。该协议被视为地区联盟格局变化以及美以对伊朗战争背景下的战略选择。

Detailed English brief

The agreement includes the deployment of Israel's David's Sling and Spyder interceptors, the Barak air and missile defense system, and multi-mission radars, along with a separate deal to add Drone Dome systems for protection against UAVs. This is the largest defense deal ever between the two countries and one of Israel's most significant export agreements in recent history. The deal comes despite Israel's current low global standing and unfavorable views among Greek citizens. Analysts attribute this move to two main factors: the growing threat of drone attacks, exemplified by a recent incident in Cyprus, and Israel's long-standing "periphery doctrine" of building ties with non-Arab states on the region's fringes. Greece seeks to bolster its defense capabilities against regional security challenges, particularly from drones and missiles, while Israel leverages its advanced defense technology to cultivate bilateral ties.

关注点 This significant defense pact between Greece and Israel underscores a strategic realignment in the Eastern Mediterranean, driven by shared security concerns, particularly the threat of drone warfare and regional instability linked to the US-Iran conflict. It highlights Israel's continued role as a major defense exporter despite geopolitical challenges and Greece's proactive approach to enhancing its military capabilities through international cooperation.
