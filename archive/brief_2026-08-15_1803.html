Lee has previously estimated Pyongyang produces 15 to 20 more warheads annually and supported a deal between U.S. President Trump and North Korean leader Kim Jong Un to freeze production, calling it a "realistic" goal while Seoul pursues full denuclearization. Coe emphasized the need for sports organizations to maintain independence and resilience against political pressure, stating that while good relationships with governments are crucial for delivering major events, politicians should not be allowed to "steer the wheel." He specifically referenced an instance where U.S. President Donald Trump reportedly called FIFA President Gianni Infantino to overturn a red card suspension for U.S. player Folarin Balogun during the World Cup, a decision FIFA subsequently reversed. Coe stressed that sports bodies must have the fortitude to "hear what you say, but thank you and goodnight" to such external influences. He also touched upon the increasing role of private equity in sports, noting that while not entirely new, it underscores the importance of organizational independence to ensure decisions serve the interests of the sport itself rather than external political or commercial agendas.

关注点 This statement from a prominent international sports leader highlights the growing tension between political influence and the autonomy of sports organizations.
