美国平等就业机会委员会（EEOC）在一份日期为2026年6月的裁决书中指出，网络技术公司思科（Cisco）可能违反了美国民权法，对其中东裔和穆斯林员工造成了敌对工作环境。此裁决源于一个名为“通往人道之桥”（Bridge to Humanity, B2H）的员工组织在2024年12月向EEOC提交的投诉。 该组织此前曾公开致信思科，呼吁公司停止向以色列政府提供技术，理由是担忧其技术被用于以色列在加沙的军事行动。该公开信获得了思科超过86,000名员工中的1,700多人签署。然而，根据员工的投诉，思科随后将这封公开信从内部网站上移除，并将其标记为“正在审查”。 投诉中详细描述了在公开信被移除后，许多签署者遭受了骚扰。其中一项指控是一位员工对另一位员工说出“别再活了”之类的言论。员工们还表示，直到他们整理了一份长达76页的报告，详细记录了在公司内部一个名为“Connected Jewish Network”的信息群组中收到的仇恨言论后，思科才开始回应他们的投诉。 根据《卫报》披露的文件，这份报告被提交给了思科的员工关系和道德办公室。报告中列举了多起仇恨言论事件，其中一起发生在2023年11月，一位匿名员工评论道：“以色列的路人今天早上杀死了巴勒斯坦一家两口人，我对此非常感激。” 报告还指出，思科员工“反复宣扬暴力，开玩笑说送人去死，并将巴勒斯坦人以及持有不同意见者比作……”。 EEOC的裁决认为，思科未能充分解决这些骚扰行为，从而可能构成了对员工民权权利的侵犯。思科方面尚未对EEOC的裁决发表公开评论。此次事件凸显了科技公司在处理内部政治敏感话题和员工权益方面的挑战。

Detailed English brief

The U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) has determined that networking technology company Cisco may have violated the civil rights of its Middle Eastern and Muslim employees by fostering a hostile work environment. This finding stems from a complaint filed in December 2024 by a Cisco employee group called "Bridge to Humanity" (B2H). B2H had previously sent an open letter to Cisco urging the company to cease providing technology to the Israeli government, citing concerns about its use in military operations. The letter was signed by over 1,700 of Cisco's more than 86,000 employees. According to the complaint, Cisco subsequently removed the letter from an internal site, labeling it "under review." Following this, many signatories allegedly faced harassment, including one reported instance where an employee told another to "quit living." The employees compiled a 76-page report detailing hate comments received in an internal messaging group called "Connected Jewish Network." This report was submitted to Cisco's Employee Relations and Ethics offices. It documented instances of employees glorifying violence and making disparaging remarks about Palestinians. One comment from November 2023 stated, "Israeli passersby killed 2 members of a Palestinian family in Jerusalem this morning, and I for one am extremely grateful." The EEOC's determination suggests Cisco did not adequately address these harassment claims, potentially violating civil rights laws. Cisco has not publicly commented on the EEOC's findings. The case highlights the challenges tech companies face in managing internal activism and employee grievances related to geopolitical conflicts.

关注点 This event is significant as it involves a major technology company, Cisco, being formally accused by a U.S. government agency of creating a hostile work environment due to anti-Arab and anti-Muslim sentiment among employees. The complaint is directly linked to internal activism regarding the company's technology sales to the Israeli military, highlighting the growing tension and legal scrutiny tech companies face when their products are perceived to be involved in international conflicts. The EEOC's finding could set a precedent for how such workplace issues are handled and may lead to increased pressure on tech firms to address employee concerns about ethical business practices and discrimination.

美国众议院通过临时拨款法案，避免政府关门至12月

US House passes funding bill to avert government shutdown ahead of midterms
Al Jazeera · 2026-09-02 06:44
详细中文报道

美国众议院于9月1日以370票对48票的压倒性多数通过了一项临时拨款法案（Continuing Resolution, CR），将联邦政府的资金延长至12月11日。这项法案此前已在参议院获得通过，现在将提交给总统特朗普签署。此举成功避免了联邦政府在2027财年开始（即10月1日）时可能面临的关门危机。 通常情况下，国会需要通过12项独立的支出法案来为联邦政府的各项计划提供全年资金，这些法案涵盖了国土安全、能源、住房和国防等多个领域。然而，截至目前，国会尚未完成2027财年（截至2027年9月30日）的全部支出法案的制定和投票。因此，这项临时拨款法案为国会议员们提供了宝贵的时间，以便在11月3日的中期选举之后，能够更从容地就全年的支出计划进行谈判和达成一致。 共和党籍的众议院拨款委员会主席汤姆·科尔在辩论中表示，“这将为国会在11月选举后争取时间”，这似乎也承认了投票季节的政治考量。政府关门在美国国内普遍不受欢迎，尤其是在选举前夕，分析人士认为这可能会影响现任议员的连任机会。 自特朗普总统第二个任期开始以来，民主党与占多数席位的共和党在预算问题上一直存在分歧。这些分歧已导致美国历史上三次政府关门，累计持续了161天。第一次关门发生在2025年10月，持续了43天，是美国历史上最长的一次，主要争议点在于医疗补助金的延期。随后在2026年1月发生了一次为期约四天的部分关门，几周后，针对国土安全部的第三次关门又发生。 此次通过的临时拨款法案，虽然只是权宜之计，但它暂时缓解了政府停摆的压力，使国会议员能够专注于其他立法议程，并为即将到来的中期选举创造一个相对稳定的政治环境。

Detailed English brief

The U.S. House of Representatives has passed a temporary funding bill, extending government operations until December 11. The measure, approved by a vote of 370 to 48, averts a potential government shutdown that could have occurred on October 1, the start of the new fiscal year. This stopgap funding, known as a continuing resolution (CR), provides Congress with additional time to negotiate the full-year spending bills for fiscal year 2027. Typically, Congress must pass 12 appropriations bills to fund federal agencies. However, these have not yet been finalized for FY2027. The passage of the CR is seen as a strategic move to avoid a shutdown before the midterm elections on November 3, as government shutdowns are historically unpopular and can negatively impact incumbents. Republican House Appropriations Committee Chairman Tom Cole acknowledged that the bill "will give Congress time to get past the November elections." This action comes after a history of budget disputes between Democrats and Republicans, which have led to three government shutdowns totaling 161 days during President Trump's second term. The longest, a 43-day shutdown in October 2025, centered on healthcare subsidies. The current CR aims to provide a stable environment for lawmakers to focus on legislative priorities and election campaigns.

关注点 The passage of this continuing resolution is significant because it temporarily resolves the immediate threat of a U.S. government shutdown ahead of crucial midterm elections. This avoids a potentially damaging political event that could have swayed voter sentiment and complicated legislative negotiations. It highlights the ongoing partisan divisions in Congress and the reliance on short-term funding measures to keep the government operational, deferring difficult budget decisions until after the elections, which could reshape the balance of power and future fiscal policy.
