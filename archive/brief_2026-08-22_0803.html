TikTok settles with US Justice Department for $400m over child privacy laws
Al Jazeera · 2026-08-22 05:59
详细中文报道

短视频应用TikTok及其母公司字节跳动（ByteDance）已与美国司法部（DOJ）就违反《儿童在线隐私保护法》（COPPA）的指控达成4亿美元的和解协议。 案件背景：美国司法部在2024年起诉TikTok和字节跳动，指控其允许13岁以下的儿童创建普通账户，并在平台上发布和分享短视频及消息，同时在未获得家长同意的情况下收集这些儿童的个人信息。COPPA法律规定，面向儿童的服务在收集13岁以下用户的个人信息前，必须获得家长的同意。 和解条款：根据协议，TikTok将立即向美国司法部支付3亿美元，并在法院取消其前身Musical.ly此前达成的和解协议后，再支付1亿美元。此次和解旨在解决对该公司违反儿童隐私法的担忧。 官方表态：美国助理总检察长Stanley E. Woodward Jr.表示：“此次和解是美国儿童和家长的重大胜利。司法部的首要任务是确保儿童在线上得到保护，并确保受托处理其个人信息的公司履行其法律义务。此次和解不仅获得了可观的赔偿，还加强了家庭所期望和应得的保护。”司法部民事司助理总检察长Brett Shumate补充说：“此次和解标志着取得了实质性进展，获得了重要的金钱赔偿，并使此事得以圆满解决。” 公司改进：司法部指出，自被起诉以来，TikTok已进行了重大改革，加强了保护儿童隐私的措施。此前，在今年1月，字节跳动同意创建一个由美国多数股权控股的合资企业，以确保美国本土数据安全，并避免美国禁止该应用（目前有超过2亿美国用户）。
下一步观察： 此次和解标志着美国政府在加强对青少年在线隐私保护方面取得重要进展。市场将继续关注TikTok在执行新隐私保护措施方面的实际效果，以及未来可能出现的类似案件和监管动向。

Detailed English brief

TikTok and its parent company ByteDance have reached a $400 million settlement with the US Department of Justice (DOJ) over allegations of violating children's online privacy laws. The DOJ sued TikTok and ByteDance in 2024, alleging that the platform knowingly allowed children under 13 to create regular accounts and collect their personal information without parental consent, in violation of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Under the terms of the settlement, TikTok will pay $300 million immediately and an additional $100 million after a previous settlement with Musical.ly is canceled by a court. Associate Attorney General Stanley E. Woodward Jr. hailed the settlement as a "major victory for American children and parents," emphasizing the department's commitment to protecting children online and ensuring companies meet their legal obligations. The DOJ noted that TikTok has implemented significant changes to strengthen child privacy safeguards since the lawsuit was filed. In January, ByteDance had also agreed to form a US-majority owned joint venture to secure domestic data and avoid a potential US ban on the app, which is used by over 200 million Americans. This resolution represents a significant step by the US government in enforcing online privacy protections for minors.

关注点 This substantial settlement highlights the increasing scrutiny on social media platforms regarding child privacy and data protection. It underscores the importance of regulatory compliance for major tech companies operating in the US and sets a precedent for future enforcement actions. The resolution also reflects the ongoing tension between technological innovation, user engagement, and the imperative to safeguard vulnerable populations online.

The regulator's intervention underscores China's stringent approach to vehicle safety and consumer protection, potentially influencing other international car manufacturers operating in the market and prompting stricter safety scrutiny globally.
