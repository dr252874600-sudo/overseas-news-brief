These twin border towns have shared close economic and social ties for a century, with many residents working, living, and shopping on both sides of the border. The tariffs not only threaten Algoma Steel's survival but also severely disrupt the economic and social fabric of this cross-border community. Local officials and business leaders have expressed deep concern over the economic fallout and are urging the government to reconsider its trade policies. Algoma Steel is currently seeking government assistance and adjusting its production strategies to navigate the crisis, but the outlook remains uncertain.
