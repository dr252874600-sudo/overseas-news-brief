全球钢铁行业协会IREPAS（国际钢铁生产商和贸易商协会）发布报告指出，当前全球范围内的高等级卷材（如热轧卷板）价格正面临显著的上涨压力。报告将此轮价格上涨的主要驱动因素归结为生产成本的持续上升。具体而言，原材料（如铁矿石、废钢）价格的波动、能源成本的增加以及物流运输费用的提高，都直接推高了钢厂的生产成本。高企的生产成本迫使钢厂在定价时需要更高的利润空间以维持运营，从而将压力传导至终端市场。IREPAS的分析师表示，这种成本驱动的价格上涨趋势可能会对依赖卷材作为关键原材料的下游产业，如汽车制造、家电生产和建筑行业，带来额外的成本负担。同时，这也可能影响全球卷材的贸易流向和市场竞争格局。目前，市场参与者正在密切关注成本变化对未来卷材价格走势的影响，以及各国政府可能采取的应对措施。

Detailed English brief

The International Steel Producers and Traders Association (IREPAS) has reported that global prices for high-grade rolled steel, such as hot-rolled coil, are facing upward pressure. The primary driver identified for this price increase is the continuous rise in production costs. Factors contributing to this include volatile raw material prices (iron ore, scrap steel), increased energy expenses, and higher logistics and transportation fees. These elevated costs necessitate higher pricing from steel mills to maintain profitability, subsequently transferring the pressure to the end market. IREPAS analysts suggest that this cost-driven price hike could impose additional financial burdens on downstream industries reliant on rolled steel, including automotive manufacturing, home appliance production, and the construction sector. Furthermore, it may influence global trade flows and market competitiveness for rolled steel. Market participants are closely monitoring the impact of cost fluctuations on future pricing trends and potential government responses.

关注点 This event highlights a significant cost-push inflation factor in the global steel market, specifically impacting high-grade rolled steel. The rising costs directly affect steel producers' margins and will likely translate into higher prices for downstream industries such as automotive, construction, and appliances. This could lead to increased manufacturing costs globally, potentially impacting consumer prices and supply chain stability. The situation warrants attention from policymakers and businesses involved in steel production, consumption, and international trade.
