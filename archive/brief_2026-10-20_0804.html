详细中文报道

详细中文报道

埃及议会已决定推迟一项关于授权其武装部队支持沙特阿拉伯的投票，原定于周一进行，现已延期至10月26日。此举延长了关于埃及在也门冲突中作用的辩论。反对党埃及社会民主党表示已通知其议员弃权，理由是议会未能获得关于此次军事任务的充分信息，包括其明确的目标、预期的持续时间、相关成本以及对埃及士兵可能带来的风险。然而，该党（拥有近十个席位）的两名议员向法新社透露，尽管存在信息不透明的问题，但其大多数议员实际上支持这项措施。议会此次的任务是授权总统阿卜杜勒-法塔赫·塞西批准沙特阿拉伯提出的任何国防援助请求，以应对也门胡塞武装对沙特的日益增加的袭击。此前，伊朗支持的胡塞武装上周袭击了利雅得国际机场和沙特国家石油公司沙特阿美。沙特当局称，10月10日的机场袭击事件造成13人死亡。塞西总统周六表示，埃及将采取“任何必要措施”来保护其海湾盟友，并强调“我们只谈论防御”。在由塞西总统主持的国家国防委员会批准后，埃及宪法要求在向国外派遣部队执行战斗任务前获得三分之二的议会多数支持。该委员会已于周日批准了该授权。埃及艾资哈尔大清真寺大伊玛目周日呼吁阿拉伯和穆斯林领导人进行调解，以避免进一步流血。沙特阿拉伯在资助埃及脆弱的经济方面发挥着重要作用，本月预计将向埃及中央银行存入53亿美元，外加2.6739亿美元的利息。周一早些时候，受可能军事介入的担忧影响，埃及EGX30股指一度下跌，但随后回升，收盘上涨0.4%。HC Securities研究主管Nematallah Choucri告诉路透社，地缘政治发展是近期市场的主要驱动因素。

Detailed English brief

Egypt's parliament has delayed a vote on authorizing its armed forces to support Saudi Arabia, adjourning the session until October 26. This move prolongs the debate over Egypt's role in the conflict in Yemen. The opposition Egyptian Social Democratic Party has instructed its lawmakers to abstain, citing insufficient information provided to parliament regarding the mission's objectives, duration, cost, and risks to Egyptian troops. Despite this, two members of parliament from the party, which holds nearly a dozen seats, told AFP that most of their lawmakers support the measure. Parliament is tasked with authorizing President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi to approve any defense assistance requests from Saudi Arabia amid increasing attacks by Yemen's Iran-aligned Houthis. The rebels had recently attacked Riyadh's international airport and Saudi Aramco. Saudi authorities reported 13 deaths in the October 10 airport attack. President el-Sisi stated on Saturday that Egypt would take "any measure" necessary to protect its Gulf allies, emphasizing "We are only talking about defense." Following approval from the National Defense Council, chaired by el-Sisi, Egypt's constitution requires a two-thirds parliamentary majority to send troops on combat missions abroad. The council gave its approval on Sunday. The Grand Imam of al-Azhar urged Arab and Muslim leaders to mediate to avert further bloodshed. Saudi Arabia plays a significant role in financing Egypt's economy, with a scheduled deposit of $5.3 billion into Egypt's central bank this month, plus interest. The Egyptian EGX30 stock index initially fell on Monday due to concerns about potential military involvement but recovered to close 0.4 percent higher. Analysts noted that geopolitical developments are a key driver of recent market movements.

关注点 This event illustrates the complex geopolitical dynamics in the Middle East and the internal considerations that influence a nation's foreign policy decisions. Egypt's potential military involvement in the Yemen conflict, at the request of Saudi Arabia, highlights the strategic importance of the relationship between the two countries, particularly given Saudi Arabia's financial support for Egypt. The parliamentary delay, driven by concerns over transparency and risk assessment, underscores the challenges of building consensus for foreign military engagements, even among allies.

Islamic finance principles strictly prohibit investments in sectors deemed unethical or harmful, including arms manufacturing. The investigation raises serious questions about HSBC's due diligence and transparency in ensuring its financial products genuinely comply with Sharia law. It highlights concerns about the destination of Muslim savings and investments and the responsibility of financial institutions in upholding their commitments to clients' religious and ethical principles. This revelation could potentially damage HSBC's reputation and may prompt more Muslim investors to scrutinize their investment choices, demanding greater transparency and accountability from financial institutions. HSBC has not yet publicly responded to the investigation's findings.

关注点 This investigation highlights a significant ethical and religious concern for Muslim investors, questioning the integrity of 'Sharia-compliant' financial products offered by major institutions like HSBC. It underscores the importance of transparency and rigorous oversight in Islamic finance to ensure alignment with religious principles and prevent investments in ethically problematic sectors, such as the military industry of a country involved in ongoing conflict.
