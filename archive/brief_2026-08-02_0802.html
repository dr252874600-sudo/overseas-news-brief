BBC Verify has found evidence suggesting the ship docked in Lomé, Togo, on July 9th, and verified footage broadcast by Malian state TV on July 25th showed a large convoy of armored vehicles and trucks arriving in the capital, Bamako, reportedly having traveled from Lomé. Satellite imagery analysis by experts indicates the ship was likely carrying vehicles similar to those seen in Bamako, including trucks, armored personnel carriers, and light infantry vehicles. Russia's Africa Corps has taken over operations previously conducted by the Wagner Group. Analysts suggest the new military hardware could bolster Malian and Russian forces to launch counteroffensives against rebel-held territories. The vessel's alleged violation of sanctions adds another layer to the geopolitical implications of this delivery.

Detailed English brief

In the first quarter of this year, Taiwan's GDP grew by an impressive 13.69%, and the second quarter saw a continued strong growth of 12.92%.
