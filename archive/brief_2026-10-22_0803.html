英国成立能源财富基金，借鉴挪威模式

The "trillion pound" oil fund the government believes Britain could have built
BBC · 2026-10-22 07:27
详细中文报道

英国政府近日宣布成立能源财富基金（Energy Wealth Fund），并从现有的国家财富基金（National Wealth Fund）中划拨20亿英镑作为初始资金。此举标志着英国在利用国家自然资源收益为未来世代积累财富方面迈出了新步伐，其灵感部分来源于挪威等国成功建立的主权财富基金。 财政部首次估算，若英国自上世纪80年代发现北海油气以来，就将相关收入进行系统性投资，并参照挪威的增长率进行复利增长，那么如今英国本可拥有一笔价值约一万亿英镑的主权财富基金。这一估算基于对英国历史上约2000亿英镑北海油气收入的投资回报模型。 财政大臣约翰·希利（John Healey）在宣布成立基金时表示：“这些油气收入现在可以为子孙后代进行投资。”他指出，如果当初就采取类似措施，英国将能建立一个巨大的国家资产。这一说法也得到了财政部内部人士的认同，他们称过去的油气收入被视为“错失的机会”，本可以系统性地投资以建立一项巨大的国家资产。 这一举措被视为对过去经济政策的某种反思。有批评者认为，撒切尔政府时期，北海油气带来的巨额收入被用于减税等短期措施，未能实现长期财富积累。而挪威则通过其庞大的主权财富基金，成功地将石油财富转化为可持续的国家投资和公共服务。 英国政府表示，将借鉴挪威的长期投资原则，但并非完全照搬其模式。新成立的能源财富基金旨在确保未来的能源收入能够得到有效管理和投资，以应对气候变化带来的挑战，并为国家经济的长期发展提供支持。此次成立基金的时机也颇具深意，正值英国国家债务首次突破3万亿英镑之际，显示出政府在寻求新的财政增长点和长期财富保值增值方面的紧迫性。

Detailed English brief

The UK government has announced the creation of an Energy Wealth Fund, diverting £2 billion from the existing National Wealth Fund to establish it. This initiative is partly inspired by Norway's successful sovereign wealth fund model, which utilizes oil and gas revenues for long-term national benefit. For the first time, Treasury calculations estimate that if the UK had systematically invested its historical North Sea oil and gas proceeds (estimated at £200 billion) since the 1980s, it could have built a sovereign wealth fund worth approximately £1 trillion today. Chancellor John Healey stated that current oil and gas revenues can now be invested for future generations, highlighting the missed opportunity of the past. This move is seen as a reflection on past economic policies, where revenues were allegedly spent on short-term tax cuts rather than long-term investment. The government aims to manage and invest future energy revenues effectively, drawing on principles of long-term investment, though not replicating Norway's model entirely. The fund's establishment occurs as the UK's national debt surpasses £3 trillion, underscoring the government's focus on securing future financial growth and wealth preservation.

关注点 The establishment of the Energy Wealth Fund marks a significant policy shift for the UK, acknowledging the potential for long-term wealth creation from natural resources, a lesson learned partly from Norway's success. The Treasury's calculation of a potential £1 trillion fund highlights a substantial missed economic opportunity from past North Sea revenues. This initiative aims to ensure future energy income contributes to national prosperity and intergenerational equity, especially pertinent given the UK's current national debt. It signals a move towards more strategic, long-term financial planning in managing national assets.
