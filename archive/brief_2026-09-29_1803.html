The model, capable of autonomously browsing the web and using applications, reportedly fell short of OpenAI's high standards regarding "staying within scope and authorisation and how it communicates back to the user about the type of work it's done," according to Saachi Jain, head of safety systems. This decision comes amid growing scrutiny of AI safety risks. OpenAI's move to pull a new release due to safety concerns is a rare instance for a major AI developer. The company emphasized its commitment to ensuring safety and alignment in model development, particularly for user-facing products.

Thousands of drivers strike in the Philippines over rising fuel prices
Al Jazeera · 2026-09-29 16:14
详细中文报道

菲律宾首都马尼拉及周边地区数千名公共交通司机于周二（9月29日）开始为期两天的罢工，抗议燃油价格持续飙升。此次罢工由运输工人联盟Piston组织，预计将有至少7万名司机和运营商参与。 罢工司机要求将油价降至每升55比索（约合0.88美元），即美国和以色列于今年2月对伊朗发动战争之前的水平。他们认为，中东地区的冲突是导致全球油价上涨的主要原因。 Piston在一份社交媒体声明中表示：“只要美国继续发动战争，油价就会持续上涨。”该组织还指责小费迪南德·马科斯总统政府和美国让司机们承受石油危机的痛苦。 另一个名为Manibela的组织此前于周一举行了罢工，要求政府提供燃油成本减免，并取消燃油的增值税和消费税。该组织已于周二暂停罢工，以便与陆路运输办公室进行对话。 菲律宾最近一次提高燃油价格是在8月25日，归因于中东地区的地缘政治冲突和敌对行动扰乱了霍尔木兹海峡等航运通道，并减少了全球石油供应。 为应对罢工可能造成的交通混乱，菲律宾国家警察署发言人表示，已部署8,643名警员协助通勤者并维持秩序。交通部也宣布将为受影响的乘客提供免费乘车服务。在东南部比科尔地区，当局也派出巴士协助受影响的通勤者。 尽管罢工组织者声称有大量司机参与，但警方表示，在马尼拉监测到的集会仅涉及275人，罢工并未对首都地区造成重大干扰。 菲律宾曾在今年3月因油价上涨宣布国家进入能源紧急状态，成为全球首个采取此举的国家。

Detailed English brief

Thousands of public transport drivers in the Philippines have begun a two-day strike protesting soaring fuel prices, demanding a reduction in costs. The transport workers' union, Piston, blames the ongoing US-Israel war against Iran for the price hikes and is calling for tax cuts on fuel. Protesters aim to have fuel prices rolled back to 55 pesos ($0.88) per liter, the level before the conflict in the Middle East began in February. Piston stated, "Fuel prices will continue to rise as long as the US persists in waging war," accusing the government of exacerbating the burden on drivers. Another group, Manibela, had previously staged a strike demanding relief from rising fuel costs and the removal of VAT and excise taxes on fuel. They have since suspended their strike to engage in dialogue with the Land Transportation Office. Recent fuel price increases in the Philippines have been attributed to geopolitical conflicts in the Middle East disrupting shipping lanes and reducing global oil supplies. The country declared a national energy emergency in March due to price increases. To mitigate the impact of the strike, the government has deployed police personnel to assist commuters and provided free bus rides. While organizers claim widespread participation, authorities report that the disruption in the capital region has not been significant.

关注点 This strike highlights the significant impact of global energy market volatility, exacerbated by geopolitical conflicts, on developing economies. The drivers' explicit linkage of local fuel prices to the US-Israel war against Iran demonstrates how international events directly influence domestic concerns and social unrest. The government's response, including deploying police and offering free transport, indicates the potential for widespread disruption and the political sensitivity of fuel prices.

Detailed English brief

Newly updated Google Maps satellite imagery reveals the devastating scale of destruction across the Gaza Strip nearly three years into what is described as Israel's "genocidal war." Neighborhoods have been reduced to rubble, making it difficult to distinguish one area from another. A recent assessment by the UN Satellite Centre (UNOSAT) indicates a 9 percent increase in destroyed structures since a "ceasefire" took effect approximately a year ago. According to UNRWA, about 82 percent of structures in Gaza, totaling around 200,000 buildings, have been damaged, with roughly two-thirds completely destroyed. Comparisons of pre-war and current satellite images show drastic changes from Rafah in the south to North Gaza. The Tal as-Sultan neighborhood in western Rafah has been almost entirely wiped out. Al-Mawasi, a coastal strip declared a "humanitarian zone" by Israel, has become overcrowded with displaced Palestinians. The images starkly illustrate the catastrophic impact of the ongoing conflict on Gaza's infrastructure and civilian population. Despite calls for a ceasefire, the humanitarian crisis continues to worsen, with the satellite data providing a grim visual testament to the destruction.

关注点 These satellite images provide undeniable visual evidence of the immense destruction in Gaza, corroborating claims of a humanitarian catastrophe. The scale of damage, with a vast majority of structures affected, underscores the devastating impact of the prolonged conflict. This visual data serves as critical evidence in ongoing international discussions and potential legal proceedings regarding the conduct of the war and its consequences for the civilian population.

塞尔维亚网球巨星诺瓦克·德约科维奇（Novak Djokovic）表示，他的身体状况良好，并对即将到来的中国网球公开赛感到兴奋。这位39岁的选手自8月30日在美国网球公开赛首轮爆冷负于马里亚诺·纳沃内（Mariano Navone）后，因肩部和背部问题以及比赛中呕吐而备受困扰，此次将是他自那场比赛以来的首次正式亮相。 德约科维奇将于本周在中国北京举行的比赛中迎战葡萄牙选手尼诺·博尔赫斯（Nuno Borges）。他表示：“当然，在正式比赛中与练习赛或练习组的感受是完全不同的。到目前为止一切都很好。我一直很期待来到中国，就像我过去一样。我在中国赛场的战绩说明了一切。知道这一点，也带来了我真正需要的信心维度。” 德约科维奇曾六次赢得中国网球公开赛冠军（2009、2010、2012、2013、2014、2015年），并且在该项ATP 500赛事中保持着29胜0负的完美记录。他承认，在中国赛场取得的辉煌历史给了他极大的信心。 这位前世界第一在过去几年里对自己的赛程安排变得越来越挑剔，他表示选择参赛的赛事更多是基于他喜欢的、过往取得成功以及受到球迷欢迎的场地。“在这个职业生涯的阶段，更多的是关于我喜欢比赛的赛事，我过去取得过成功的赛事，以及我感觉受到球迷积极欢迎的赛事，”德约科维奇说。“我在中国赛场的历史战绩令人难以置信。我已经11年没有来这里比赛了。过去几年我一直在努力安排赛程，但最终只打了上海的比赛。今年，北京是我的优先考虑名单之一。” 德约科维奇的目标仍然是追求创纪录的第25个大满贯冠军头衔，这是他职业生涯中为数不多的几项重要目标之一。他表示，他希望通过在中国赛场的良好表现来找回比赛的节奏和信心。

Detailed English brief

Tennis superstar Novak Djokovic has expressed optimism about his physical condition and his return to the China Open. The 39-year-old Serbian player, who has been struggling with shoulder and back issues, including vomiting during his shock first-round exit at the US Open on August 30, will compete for the first time since that match. He is scheduled to face Portugal's Nuno Borges in Beijing on Wednesday. Djokovic, a six-time champion at the China Open, holds an undefeated 29-0 record at the ATP 500 event. He stated, "Obviously it’s completely different when you’re playing an official match compared to practice sets or practice sessions. So far so good. I was looking forward to coming to China, as I always have. My record speaks for itself on Chinese soil. Knowing that, it also brings this dimension of confidence that I really need." He acknowledged that his "incredible" history of success in China provides a significant confidence boost. Djokovic has become more selective about his tournament schedule in recent years, prioritizing events he enjoys and where he has a strong track record and positive fan reception. "At this stage of my career, it’s really also about the tournaments where I enjoy to play, where I’ve had the success in the past, where I feel like I’m also welcomed by the fans in a positive light," he said. Having not played in Beijing for 11 years, Djokovic confirmed that the China Open was a priority for him this year, unlike previous years where he only played the Shanghai Masters. His primary goal remains pursuing a record-extending 25th Grand Slam title, and he hopes his return to China will help him regain momentum.
