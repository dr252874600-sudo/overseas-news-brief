Former US President Donald Trump has intervened in the leadership of tech giant Nvidia, reportedly convincing the company's board to reinstate CEO Jensen Huang, who had resigned less than two weeks prior due to revelations of extramarital affairs, one allegedly involving a suspected Chinese spy. Trump claimed he "made the deal" to get Huang his job back, a move that has drawn backlash from lawmakers and the tech industry. Despite the controversy, Huang's return on November 23rd (local time) saw Nvidia's market value surge by nearly $1 trillion, with its stock price jumping 31%, reversing a losing streak and boosting Asian markets. However, analysts note that the closing price was still down from its peak, indicating investor concerns about the broader AI industry and future development. Huang is scheduled to appear before Congress next week to address national security concerns related to his alleged ties with Alice Huang, who was identified as a suspected Chinese spy and whose company, Megaspeed, is reportedly being considered for blacklisting by the US Commerce Department. Nvidia's board stated that Huang's reinstatement was in the "best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers" and that they had received input from senior levels of the US government, assuring no national security risk. China has denied the spying allegations.

也门政府军在阿尔拜达省对胡塞武装的攻势陷入停滞

Why has Yemen’s offensive against the Houthis in al-Bayda stalled?
Al Jazeera · 2026-11-24 15:36
详细中文报道

也门政府军于9月在战略要地阿尔拜达省（al-Bayda）对胡塞武装发起的反攻，在取得初步进展后陷入停滞。阿尔拜达省位于也门中部，是自胡塞武装9月在沿海和塔伊兹周边取得进展以来，冲突的一个主要焦点。尽管此后胡塞武装在阿尔拜达的阵地遭受了数百次空袭，但地面部队未能实现更大规模的推进，未能向省会城市取得实质性突破。据报道，胡塞武装的防御工事、雷区以及崎岖的地形延缓了政府军的攻势。与此同时，政府军在也门其他地区也面临作战压力。11月21日，胡塞武装媒体报道称，其在阿尔拜达省的穆凯拉斯区（Mukayras）的电信塔遭到8次空袭。11月中旬，政府军为了加强对政府主要行动基地亚丁（Aden）的防御，从战略重要的曼德海峡（Bab al-Mandeb Strait）附近区域撤兵。阿尔拜达省位于萨那（Sana'a）东南方向，连接也门南北，占据着重要的战略位置，并包含数个关键军事前沿。对胡塞武装而言，控制该省可作为防御缓冲，并提供连接邻近地区补给线的通道。对政府军而言，收复阿尔拜达省将能切断胡塞武装的补给线，缓解周边省份的压力，并可能打开通往胡塞武装自2014年9月以来占领的首都萨那的路线。然而，该省多山的地形、蜿蜒的道路和山谷使得进攻部队难以推进、巩固领土和维持补给线。也门军事分析家穆罕默德·库迈姆（Mohammed al-Kumaim）准将此前告诉半岛电视台，如果政府军控制阿尔拜达省，将能切断胡塞武装的补给线并增加其压力。

Detailed English brief

The Yemeni government forces' counteroffensive against the Houthi rebels in the strategically important al-Bayda province, launched in September, has stalled after initial gains. The offensive has failed to achieve a significant breakthrough, reportedly due to Houthi fortifications, minefields, and difficult terrain, coupled with pressure on government forces fighting on other fronts. Al-Bayda, located in central Yemen, has become a flashpoint in the conflict. Despite hundreds of air raids on Houthi positions in the province since September, ground advances have not developed into a broader push towards the provincial capital. In mid-November, government-aligned forces withdrew from areas near the Bab al-Mandeb Strait to reinforce defenses around Aden, the government's main base. Al-Bayda's mountainous terrain and winding roads make it challenging for attacking forces to advance and maintain supply lines. Retaking al-Bayda is strategically crucial for the government as it could disrupt Houthi supply lines, ease pressure on surrounding provinces, and potentially open routes towards the capital, Sanaa. Houthi media reported eight air strikes on telecommunications towers in al-Bayda's Mukayras district on November 21.

关注点 The stalled offensive in al-Bayda highlights the protracted and complex nature of the Yemeni civil war. The strategic importance of al-Bayda province means its control is vital for both sides, impacting supply lines and potential advances. The difficulties faced by the government forces underscore the Houthi's resilience and the challenging operational environment. The situation also reflects the broader strain on government forces, necessitating troop redeployments from other critical areas like the Bab al-Mandeb Strait, which has global shipping implications.

Sheikh Hasina plans to return to Bangladesh from exile on December 16
Al Jazeera · 2026-11-24 15:35
详细中文报道

孟加拉国前总理谢赫·哈西娜（Sheikh Hasina）的发言人赛义德·穆扎默尔·阿里（Syed Mozammel Ali）于11月24日表示，哈西娜计划于12月16日，即孟加拉国1971年独立战争结束纪念日，结束在印度的流亡生涯返回国内。哈西娜于2024年8月在一场大规模学生抗议活动后被迫结束了长达15年的执政并流亡印度。随后，孟加拉国一家法院缺席判处她死刑，罪名是下令镇压导致数百名抗议者死亡的起义。哈西娜否认了这一判决，并表示一旦回国将准备应诉。她呼吁现任总理塔里克·拉赫曼（Tarique Rahman）安排她乘坐孟加拉国国家航空公司（Biman Bangladesh Airlines）的飞机从德里返回达卡。然而，拉赫曼总理的新闻秘书阿布·阿卜杜拉·萨利赫（Abu Abdullah M Saleh）在接受美联社采访时重申，政府的立场非常明确，即哈西娜作为一名被定罪的罪犯，只能通过引渡方式回国。预计哈西娜回国后将被逮捕并送往监狱。孟加拉国国际罪行法庭的首席检察官阿米努尔·伊斯兰（Aminul Islam）认为，一名被定罪的逃犯要求政府安排其回国是不合理的。此前，司法部门曾表示，哈西娜因错过了上诉的法定期限，将无法就死刑判决提出上诉，也没有法律依据阻止其执行。在孟加拉国，总统可以进行特赦，但哈西娜此前从未表示过寻求特赦，并坚决否认判决。联合国称，导致哈西娜下台的骚乱期间，有多达1400人丧生。哈西娜的父亲是孟加拉国独立战争的领导人，并在独立后成为新孟加拉国的第一任领导人。

Detailed English brief

Former Prime Minister of Bangladesh Sheikh Hasina plans to return to her country from exile in India on December 16, the anniversary of the end of Bangladesh's 1971 liberation war, her spokesperson announced on November 24. Hasina went into exile in August 2024 after a student-led uprising forced her to end her 15-year rule. She was subsequently sentenced to death in absentia by a Bangladesh court for ordering the deadly crackdown on the protests, which killed hundreds of demonstrators. Hasina has rejected the verdict and stated her readiness to fight the case upon her return. She has urged Prime Minister Tarique Rahman to arrange her flight back to Dhaka on the national airline. However, the government maintains its stance that Hasina, as a convicted individual, can only return through extradition. Prime Minister Rahman's press secretary reiterated that the government's position is clear and they have sought her extradition. Hasina is expected to be arrested and jailed upon arrival. The chief prosecutor of Bangladesh's International Crimes Tribunal deemed it unreasonable for a convicted fugitive to expect the government to facilitate her return. The judiciary had previously stated that Hasina would be unable to appeal her death sentence due to missing the deadline, leaving no legal basis to halt her execution. The UN estimates that up to 1,400 people were killed during the unrest that led to Hasina's ouster.

关注点 Sheikh Hasina's planned return marks a significant development in Bangladeshi politics, potentially leading to a major confrontation with the current government. Her conviction and death sentence in absentia, coupled with the government's insistence on extradition, highlight the deep political divisions and legal complexities. Her return could reignite political tensions and influence the country's future political landscape, especially given her past influence and the circumstances of her departure from power. The case also raises questions about the rule of law and political retribution in Bangladesh.
