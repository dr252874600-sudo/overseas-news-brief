AI代币经济学：定价与成本控制的挑战

Tokenomics: Why making AI pay is tricky
BBC、The New York Times · 2026-08-04 07:21
详细中文报道

人工智能（AI）服务的定价和成本控制正成为一个日益严峻的挑战，催生了一个名为“代币经济学”（tokenomics）的新兴领域。大型AI公司如微软、谷歌和Anthropic在开发大型语言模型（LLM）方面投入了巨额资金，并推出了包含额外功能的付费版本，以期收回投资。与此同时，第三方公司正在构建和销售基于AI代理的服务，这些服务通常基于LLM，并被训练来执行特定任务。然而，为这些服务设定价格却异常困难。Saviynt公司的Simon Gooch指出，试图与客户签订为期12个月、24个月或36个月的成本模型是不切实际的，因为“我们不知道（未来成本）”。 这种不确定性的根源在于构成LLM和AI代理基础的“代币”的经济学正在迅速变化。当用户向LLM（如ChatGPT或Claude）提出请求时，这些请求会被分解成称为“代币”的数学单元，以便模型进行处理。模型的响应也以代币的形式产生，然后被转换回文本、代码或自动化指令。然而，这个过程并非完全可预测。提示的细微变化可能导致不同的答案，即使是相同的提示也可能不会每次都产生相同的结果，不同的模型也会产生不同的输出。在AI代理系统中，企业使用多个AI代理协同工作以做出决策和采取行动，这进一步增加了代币的使用量和不可预测性。根据高盛的分析，尽管近年来单个代币的成本（或用于支付代币的积分）已大幅下降，但企业消耗的代币数量却在增加，这使得成本模型难以建立和维持。

Detailed English brief

The pricing and cost control of Artificial Intelligence (AI) services are emerging as significant challenges, giving rise to a new field known as "tokenomics." Major AI firms like Microsoft, Google, and Anthropic have invested heavily in developing Large Language Models (LLMs) and offer paid versions with enhanced features to recoup their investments. Concurrently, third-party companies are building and selling services based on AI agents, typically built on LLMs and trained for specific tasks. However, setting prices for these services is proving remarkably difficult. Simon Gooch from Saviynt states that attempting to lock customers into cost models for 12, 24, or 36 months is unrealistic because "we don't know" future costs. The unpredictability stems from the rapidly changing economics of "tokens," the fundamental units of LLMs and AI agents. When a user prompts an LLM, the request is broken down into tokens for processing, and the response is also generated in tokens. This process is not entirely predictable; subtle prompt variations can lead to different answers, and even the same prompt may not yield identical results across different models or even the same model at different times. In agentic systems, multiple AI agents working together further increase token usage and uncertainty. While the cost per token has plummeted, the overall volume of tokens consumed is rising, making cost modeling a complex endeavor for both AI providers and their clients.

关注点 This event highlights a critical bottleneck in the widespread adoption and monetization of AI. The unpredictability of token usage makes it difficult for businesses to budget for AI services and for AI providers to offer stable pricing. This uncertainty could slow down investment and innovation in the AI sector if not addressed.
