中东冲突升级引发油价波动，壳牌利润翻倍

Oil Prices Teeter as Middle East Veers Toward Wider Conflict
The New York Times、BBC、The Economic Times · 2026-07-30 17:44
详细中文报道

美国与伊朗之间冲突的升级，特别是对霍尔木兹海峡石油和液化天然气（LNG）全球供应造成的严重干扰，已导致油价大幅上涨。据报道，壳牌公司（Shell）在2026年第二季度（4月至6月）的利润飙升至98.4亿美元，较去年同期的42.6亿美元翻了一番多。这主要得益于油价的上涨以及公司交易业务从能源价格的剧烈波动中获利。 壳牌首席执行官瓦埃尔·萨万（Wael Sawan）表示，公司强劲的运营表现使得在动荡的全球能源市场中取得了优异的业绩。今年上半年，壳牌的盈利增长了70%。与其他大型能源公司如英国石油（BP）和挪威国家石油公司（Equinor）类似，壳牌也受益于能源价格的波动。 冲突爆发前，布伦特原油价格约为每桶73美元，此后曾一度超过120美元，但随着对霍尔木兹海峡何时重开的猜测，价格也回落至100美元以下。这些价格的大幅波动为交易商提供了更宽的利润空间。 然而，中东冲突也对壳牌的部分业务造成了影响。其在卡塔尔的LNG生产自3月初以来因冲突而停产，位于卡塔尔的珍珠气化制油（Pearl GTL）工厂在3月份的一次导弹袭击中遭受了“大范围损坏”，预计修复时间约为一年。因此，壳牌第二季度的天然气日产量从第一季度的90.9万桶油当量降至63.1万桶油当量。今年上半年，壳牌的总油气产量同比下降了16%。尽管如此，公司也提到了在巴西和美国墨西哥湾的新增石油产量。 在金融市场方面，美国股市在美伊冲突暂停的消息后出现上涨。然而，市场参与者正密切关注美联储的动向以及即将公布的企业财报，这些因素可能影响未来的市场走势。同时，对通胀的担忧也推高了债券收益率。

Detailed English brief

Escalating conflict between the US and Iran, particularly the significant disruption to global oil and liquefied natural gas (LNG) supplies through the Strait of Hormuz, has led to a sharp rise in oil prices. Shell reported a more than doubling of its second-quarter profits to $9.84 billion (£7.37 billion) for April-June, up from $4.26 billion a year earlier. The company's trading operations also benefited from the volatility in energy prices, contributing to a 70% surge in first-half earnings. Shell CEO Wael Sawan highlighted the company's strong operational performance amidst severe disruption in global energy markets. Before the conflict, Brent crude oil prices hovered around $73 a barrel, peaking above $120 and later falling below $100 as speculation about the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz intensified. These price swings typically widen the gap between buying and selling prices, enabling traders to make larger profits. However, the Middle East conflict has impacted some of Shell's operations. Its LNG production in Qatar has been shut down since early March due to the conflict, and its Pearl GTL facility in Qatar suffered extensive damage from a missile attack in March, with repairs expected to take about a year. Consequently, Shell's overall gas production fell in the second quarter. Despite a 16% year-on-year drop in total oil and gas production for the first half of the year, Shell noted new oil production in Brazil and the Gulf of America. In financial markets, US stocks climbed following news of a pause in the US-Iran conflict. However, market participants are closely watching the Federal Reserve and upcoming corporate earnings reports, which could influence future market movements. Bond yields have also risen on concerns about inflation.

关注点 The escalating conflict in the Middle East has direct and significant implications for global energy markets, impacting oil prices, corporate profits of major energy companies like Shell, and potentially influencing broader economic stability through inflation concerns and market volatility. The disruption to key shipping lanes like the Strait of Hormuz underscores the geopolitical risks to energy supply chains.

Detailed English brief

UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham has expressed concerns that the increasing reliance on virtual job interviews may hinder young candidates from showcasing their personality and passion. Speaking on a careers podcast, Burnham stated his dislike for the current culture of conducting interviews via platforms like Zoom or Teams, questioning how a young person can "shine" in such a setting. He worries that technology, including AI, used in recruitment might not be making the process fairer, potentially disadvantaging those who possess strong personal qualities but struggle with formulaic answers. This concern comes at a time when over one million young people aged 16 to 25 are not in employment, education, or training, and job vacancies are at a five-year low. Data from LinkedIn indicates that 89% of UK recruiters plan to increase their use of AI in hiring this year. Some young people have described the experience of applying for numerous jobs without hearing back as "robotic and brutal." Burnham's remarks align with a recent major review by former minister Alan Milburn, which warned of a "lost generation" if action is not taken, as job and career opportunities for young people are shrinking. The review found that one in six young adults could be out of work, education, or training within five years. In a related policy suggestion, Burnham mentioned that companies offering six-week work placements for teenagers could be prioritized for government contracts.

关注点 This highlights a growing debate about the impact of technology, particularly AI and virtual communication, on fairness and opportunity in the job market, especially for young people. It touches upon broader issues of youth unemployment, the changing nature of work, and the potential for technology to exacerbate existing inequalities if not implemented thoughtfully.
