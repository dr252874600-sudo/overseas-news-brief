In contrast, while Vietnam has attracted significant Chinese investment over the past decade, it is now facing challenges such as rising labor costs, infrastructure bottlenecks, and over-reliance on foreign investment. Furthermore, Vietnam contends with greater geopolitical pressures in managing its relations with both the U.S. and China. The trend of Chinese enterprises favoring Thailand reflects their pursuit of safer and more stable production and operational bases amidst global supply chain restructuring and increasing geopolitical uncertainties. This strategic shift could also have profound implications for the regional economic landscape.

Detailed English brief

The Gambia Reparations Commission pledged to manage the funds responsibly, transparently, and accountably, prioritizing the rights and needs of victims and survivors. Jammeh, currently residing in Equatorial Guinea, was ousted in 2017 following his election loss and pressure from neighboring West African nations. He faces accusations of ordering the murder of political opponents, disappearances, rape, and torture, with an estimated 240 to 250 victims killed during his regime. The U.S. Justice Department had filed a civil forfeiture complaint in 2020 alleging the property was bought with proceeds of corruption, and a court ordered its forfeiture in 2022. The U.S. Justice Department announced in September that it would return the sale proceeds to The Gambia. President Adama Barrow stated the government will adhere to official auditors' recommendations for allocating the reparations.

关注点 This event demonstrates a significant step towards transitional justice for victims of authoritarian rule.
