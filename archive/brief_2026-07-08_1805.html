Consequently, over 500,000 Russians declared bankruptcy last year, a nearly one-third year-on-year increase. Russia's Ministry of Economic Development has also lowered its 2026 GDP growth forecast to 0.4% from 1.3%. Concurrently, Russia is experiencing a severe fuel shortage crisis. Long queues at gas stations are common, even in Moscow, with some stations running out of fuel entirely. Citizens express frustration over rising prices and difficulties in obtaining fuel, impacting travel plans. While some attribute the issue to poor preparation and Ukrainian attacks on oil refineries, the crisis highlights the war's tangible impact on daily life.
