Al Jazeera · 2026-09-27 05:51
详细中文报道

美国总统特朗普于周六通过社交媒体宣布，将推翻前任总统拜登时期制定的燃油经济性标准。特朗普声称，新标准将“终止”他所称的拜登政府的“电动汽车（EV）强制令”，并指责前政府给汽车制造商带来了高昂成本，并强制引导消费者购买电动汽车。他表示，新标准将“去除在美国制造汽车的浪费”，从而“降低价格”，为家庭节省数千美元购买新车。此举是特朗普政府扭转拜登政府支持低排放汽车政策的一部分，进一步拉大了美国政策与全球电动汽车发展趋势之间的差距。报道指出，尽管特朗普和共和党人使用“电动汽车强制令”来描述拜登政府提高燃油效率的要求，但实际上美国联邦层面并没有强制购买电动汽车或禁止销售燃油车的法律。核心在于企业平均燃油经济性（CAFE）标准，该标准要求汽车制造商达到其销售的全部汽车和轻型卡车的平均燃油经济性目标。拜登政府在2024年最终确定的规则要求到2031年车队平均燃油经济性从39.1英里/加仑提高到约50.4英里/加仑，旨在减少燃料消耗和排放以应对气候变化。作为回应，汽车制造商已增加电动汽车产量以满足要求。然而，共和党控制的国会去年通过的《一项大而美的法案》削减了电动汽车的消费者税收抵免。

Detailed English brief

US President Donald Trump announced on Saturday that he will roll back fuel economy standards set during the previous administration of President Joe Biden. Trump stated on social media that the new standards will "TERMINATE" what he called the Biden administration's "EV (electric vehicle) mandate," accusing the former administration of imposing costly requirements on automakers and steering consumers towards electric vehicles. He asserted that the new standards will "take the waste out of building cars in America," leading to "LOWER PRICES" and saving families thousands of dollars on new vehicles. This move is part of a broader effort by the Trump administration to reverse Biden-era policies supporting lower-emission vehicles, widening the gap between US policy and global trends favoring electric vehicles. The report clarifies that while Trump and Republicans refer to "EV mandate," there are no federal laws requiring Americans to buy electric cars or prohibiting the sale of gasoline-powered ones. The core issue lies with Corporate Average Fuel Economy (CAFE) standards, which require automakers to meet average fuel economy targets across their fleet. The Biden administration's rules, finalized in 2024, aimed to increase fleetwide fuel economy from 39.1 mpg to approximately 50.4 mpg by 2031 to reduce fuel consumption and emissions. In response, manufacturers increased EV production. However, tax credits for electric vehicles were reduced by Congress last year.

关注点 This policy shift by the Trump administration signals a potential rollback of environmental regulations aimed at promoting fuel efficiency and electric vehicle adoption. It could have significant implications for the automotive industry, consumer choices, and the US's commitment to climate change mitigation, potentially creating divergence with international efforts to transition to cleaner transportation.

Reports indicate the government plans to approve a decree to accelerate migrant returns from Ceuta to Morocco.

Taiwan's 'silicon shield' strategy, leveraging its dominance in the global semiconductor supply chain to deter potential military action from mainland China, has become particularly crucial in the age of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Companies like TSMC produce advanced chips essential for global AI development, making Taiwan strategically vital to the United States and its allies. Despite China's significant investments in its semiconductor industry, Taiwan maintains a substantial lead in advanced process nodes that is difficult to overcome in the short term. This technological indispensability, coupled with geopolitical tensions, underscores Taiwan's delicate position. US reliance on Taiwan has led to increased commitments to its security and efforts to diversify supply chains, but Taiwan's 'silicon shield' effect remains potent for now. However, this dependence also presents a potential risk, as any conflict in Taiwan could have devastating consequences for the global AI industry and economy.
