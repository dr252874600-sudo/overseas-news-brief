Detailed English brief

The attack resulted in at least 29 deaths, 10 injuries, and 48 missing persons. President Volodymyr Zelensky described the attack as "utterly horrific" and stated that Russian forces could not have been unaware that the site was where local residents received humanitarian aid weekly. He suggested this was further proof that Russia does not desire de-escalation and is signaling a preference for further killings. Reports indicate the first bombs hit a local school used for humanitarian needs, followed by a second strike on the shelter location. Earlier, Ukraine's State Emergency Service (DSNS) reported 24 confirmed deaths, including two rescuers and four police officers. Search and rescue operations are ongoing, with fears that the death toll may rise. The Russian military has not publicly commented on the incident. This attack follows a recent successful joint Russian-Ukrainian operation to evacuate civilians from the Russian-occupied southern city of Oleshky, which Zelensky had seen as a potential sign that peace was possible.

关注点 This event represents a significant escalation in civilian casualties in the Ukraine conflict, directly targeting a location providing humanitarian aid. 英欧就脱欧后食品出口“红线”达成协议草案

BBC · 2026-11-03 17:55
详细中文报道
