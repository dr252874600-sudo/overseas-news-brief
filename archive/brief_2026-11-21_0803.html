关注点 This event highlights a potential shift in US tech policy under the Trump administration, focusing on domestic AI infrastructure development and national security concerns related to China's technological advancement. The threat of significant taxes on overseas data centers could impact global tech investment and supply chains, particularly in regions like Southeast Asia. The lack of clarity on the policy's details and its surprise announcement to cabinet members underscore the unpredictable nature of such initiatives and their potential implications for the tech industry.

乌克兰战争对野生动物造成毁灭性影响

The War Is Ravaging Ukraine’s Wildlife, Too
The New York Times · 2026-11-21 07:05
详细中文报道

俄罗斯对乌克兰的战争已进入第五个年头，除了众所周知的人道主义灾难外，也对该国丰富的野生动物及其栖息地造成了毁灭性的影响。乌克兰拥有约70,000种动植物，占欧洲生物多样性的35%。然而，战争导致了难以精确统计的野生动物死亡。据报道，海滩上冲上来许多海豚和鼠海豚的残骸。无人机碎片引发的火灾烧毁了数千英亩的森林栖息地。在乌克兰东部顿巴斯地区，数英里长的反无人机网以及用于操作无人机的光纤电缆，缠住了成千上万的鸟类、鹿、狐狸等动物，其中只有少数能够获救。全国范围内的火灾，由炮击、燃烧弹、地雷和被击落无人机的碎片引起，摧毁了野生动物的栖息地并迫使它们迁徙。虽然数百万头牲畜在战争中死亡，但野生动物的损失数字无法精确追踪。一些在战争中受伤的动物得到了救援，但大多数动物未能幸免。报道指出，乌克兰的自然景观从古老的松树林到广阔的草原和湿地，都因战争而遭受重创。

Detailed English brief

The war in Ukraine, now in its fifth year, has had devastating consequences for the country's rich wildlife and their habitats, in addition to the well-known human toll. Ukraine is home to an estimated 70,000 plant and animal species, accounting for 35% of Europe's biodiversity. However, the war has led to an immeasurable loss of wildlife. Reports indicate that the remains of dolphins and porpoises have been washing up on shores. Fires started by drone debris have burned thousands of acres of forest habitat. In Ukraine's eastern Donbas region, thousands of miles of anti-drone netting and fiber-optic cables used for drone operations have ensnared countless birds, deer, and foxes, with only a lucky few being rescued. Nationwide fires, ignited by artillery strikes, incendiary weapons, land mines, and debris from shot-down drones, have destroyed habitats and displaced wildlife. While millions of livestock are known to have been killed, the number of wild animals lost is impossible to track with precision. Some injured animals have been rescued, but most have not been so fortunate. The country's landscapes, ranging from ancient pine forests to vast steppes and wetlands, have been severely impacted by the conflict.

关注点 This report highlights a critical but often overlooked consequence of the war in Ukraine: its severe impact on biodiversity. The destruction of habitats, direct harm to wildlife, and the long-term ecological damage underscore the multifaceted devastation caused by the conflict. It emphasizes the need for post-war conservation efforts and raises awareness about the broader environmental costs of armed conflict.
