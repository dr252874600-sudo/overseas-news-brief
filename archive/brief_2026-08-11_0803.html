BBC · 2026-08-11 05:33
详细中文报道

英国首相安迪·伯纳姆宣布了一系列旨在改善英国城镇中心形象的计划，核心内容是加强地方政府的权力，以控制电子烟店和博彩商店的扩张。首相指出，数十年的衰退已导致许多城镇中心“被掏空”，变得面目全非。 根据新计划，所有新开设的电子烟店（仅限英格兰地区）将需要向当地议会申请开业许可。同时，政府将收紧对博彩商店的定义，防止商家通过将其注册为便利店或零售店来规避现有规定。此外，英国政府还将废除一项名为“旨在许可”（aim to permit）的规则，该规则此前限制了地方议会在拒绝开设新的博彩商店和24小时老虎机商店方面的能力（适用于整个英国）。对于成人游戏中心（提供24小时赌博机服务的场所），在英格兰地区也将需要规划许可。 这些措施的出台背景是，英国国家犯罪局估计，每年至少有10亿英镑的非法资金通过高街商店进行洗钱，这些商店涉及销售假冒商品、逃税、非法用工和非法毒品供应等活动。此前，BBC的调查也揭露了高街上存在的有组织犯罪问题，包括销售非法香烟和电子烟、大麻和可卡因，以及涉嫌洗钱等。 首相表示，这些新规将赋予地方政府更大的控制权，决定哪些企业可以在城镇中心开业。然而，保守党和改革党对此表示批评，认为这些措施将导致“更多空置”的店铺，并且没有为其他小型企业提供税收减免。 此次宣布是首相正在进行的全国巡访的一部分，他表示将在访问中“倾听”，并制定一项“10年计划以重拾希望”。

Detailed English brief

UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham has announced new measures to grant local councils greater powers to control the proliferation of vape shops and betting shops in town centers. The aim is to revitalize high streets, which Burnham described as having been "hollowed out" by decades of decline. Under the proposed changes, new vape shops in England will require planning permission. The definition of a vape shop will be tightened to prevent businesses from circumventing rules by registering as general retailers. A rule known as "aim to permit," which limited councils' ability to refuse new betting shops, will be scrapped across Great Britain. Planning permission will also be required for new adult gaming centers in England. These proposals come amid concerns about organized crime and illicit activities on high streets, with the National Crime Agency estimating that at least £1 billion in criminal cash is laundered annually through such businesses. The BBC has previously exposed issues including the sale of illegal goods and suspected money laundering. However, the Conservative and Reform parties have criticized the plans, arguing they could lead to more empty storefronts without offering tax relief to other small businesses. The Prime Minister is undertaking a UK tour as part of a broader initiative to develop a "10-year plan to bring back hope."

关注点 This policy shift reflects a growing concern in the UK about the impact of certain retail businesses on the character and safety of town centers. By empowering local authorities, the government aims to address issues ranging from urban decay to organized crime, potentially setting a precedent for similar regulatory approaches in other areas or countries facing comparable challenges.

Detailed English brief

Nvidia has secured $500 billion in capital from major Wall Street banks and investors, including Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR, to develop artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure. This significant funding round marks the first time investors are treating AI hardware and infrastructure, often referred to as "compute," as a distinct asset class. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stated, "In AI, compute is revenue. We are bringing the world's leading long-term capital providers together to independently underwrite AI infrastructure." The capital will support both Nvidia's internal projects and those of its partners. Projects funded by this initiative are expected to include the construction of new data centers designed to house, operate, and cool the vast arrays of stacked computer chips essential for processing AI data and executing AI tasks. Additionally, the funding will back new factories aimed at manufacturing the AI chips required to power these systems and increase their availability to customers. KKR co-CEOs Joe Bae and Scott Nuttall commented, "Compute has become a critical infrastructure asset." They added that "delivery, not ambition, is the hard part" in scaling digital infrastructure. Virtually all major technology and AI companies rely on Nvidia's chips (GPUs) for their services. Companies like Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, SpaceX, Tesla, OpenAI, and Anthropic have collectively spent over $1 trillion on AI projects and infrastructure in the last three years, with further spending anticipated. This demand has quintupled Nvidia's market value in the same period.

关注点 This deal represents a massive influx of capital into AI infrastructure, underscoring the immense growth and investment potential in the AI sector. It highlights Nvidia's central role in the AI ecosystem and signals a new investment paradigm where AI compute is recognized as a core asset class. The funding will accelerate the development of data centers and chip manufacturing, potentially impacting the pace of AI adoption and innovation globally.
