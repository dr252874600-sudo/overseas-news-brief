Detailed English brief

He already faces charges of breach of trust and self-dealing. Prosecutors announced the new charge on Friday, adding to existing accusations. Peña is accused of using his presidential position to direct millions in government funds to Ueno and concealing its financial troubles despite warnings about risky lending practices, while having financial ties to the bank. He held a stake in the bank's holding company until last year. Investigators raided his official residence, and authorities are seeking to locate and freeze assets that have been moved abroad, with requests made to the US, Luxembourg, and Argentina. Three former central bank officials were also arrested, accused of being aware of Ueno's issues since late last year. The bank, which handled over $800 million in public funds, grew rapidly during Peña's presidency.

关注点 This event highlights potential corruption and financial mismanagement at the highest levels of government in Paraguay, with significant implications for the country's financial stability and public trust.
