美国动态： 美国总统特朗普于周六（9月26日）表示，伊朗提出的结束美伊战争的最新方案“不可接受”。此前，伊朗在联合国大会上提出一项协议：若美国解除对伊朗的制裁、释放被冻结的伊朗资金并结束对伊朗港口的军事封锁，伊朗将在七天内重新开放霍尔木兹海峡，并重返关于其核计划的谈判。
伊朗动态： 伊朗方面表示仍在等待华盛顿的“明确消息”，并提出了上述旨在缓解紧张局势的方案。该方案的核心是希望通过外交途径解决争端，并恢复关键的石油运输通道。 海峡航运： 霍尔木兹海峡是连接波斯湾与阿曼湾及阿拉伯海的重要海上通道，在美伊战争爆发前，约占全球石油供应量的五分之一。自战争开始以来，该水道的商业航运量急剧下降，原因包括对船只的袭击事件频发，多数袭击被归咎于伊朗或其盟友。根据海事情报平台MarineTraffic的数据，9月21日至27日期间，该海峡的船只通行次数为132次，较前一周的116次有所上升，但远低于战争爆发前每日约130次的水平。 市场反应： 油价上涨的同时，亚洲股市表现不一。日本和韩国股市下跌，而香港和澳大利亚股市则小幅上涨。华尔街在上周五收盘时表现强劲。
下一步观察： 市场密切关注美伊双方是否会重启更具建设性的对话，以及任何可能影响石油供应的进一步军事或外交动向。霍尔木兹海峡的航运安全和通畅程度将是影响油价的关键因素。

Detailed English brief

Brent crude futures approached $108 a barrel, marking an increase of over 3 percent during Asian trading hours. U.S. President Donald Trump rejected Iran's proposal over the weekend, deeming it "unacceptable." The Iranian proposal, announced at the UN General Assembly, offered to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days in exchange for the U.S. releasing frozen Iranian funds, lifting sanctions, and ending its naval blockade on Iranian ports, alongside a return to nuclear negotiations. The Strait of Hormuz, which accounts for about one-fifth of global oil supplies, has seen a significant decline in commercial shipping since the war began due to frequent attacks on vessels, many attributed to Iran or its allies. Maritime intelligence platform MarineTraffic reported 132 transits of the strait between September 21-27, an increase from the previous week but still far below the pre-war average of approximately 130 daily crossings. Asian stock markets showed mixed performance amidst the volatility, with Japan and South Korea declining while Hong Kong and Australia saw modest gains. Market participants are closely monitoring for any signs of renewed constructive dialogue between the U.S. and Iran and potential further military or diplomatic developments impacting oil supply.

关注点 The ongoing tensions between the US and Iran, coupled with the rejection of a proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, directly impact global oil prices and shipping routes, affecting the international economy. The situation highlights the fragility of energy markets and the significant geopolitical risks associated with the conflict.
