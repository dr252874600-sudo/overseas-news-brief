Detailed English brief

Hawkins claimed that since early May, US forces have facilitated the passage of over 900 million barrels of crude oil through the strait to the global energy market. He asserted that US forces are simultaneously conducting two missions: facilitating commercial traffic and enforcing a blockade, having redirected more than 100 ships attempting to violate it. Hawkins also reported that US forces had completed mine-clearing operations in the main shipping lanes several weeks prior and remain vigilant to keep them clear. He insisted, "The strait is open. Those lanes are open." These comments come amid heightened tensions over the vital waterway, with competing claims over control between the US and Iran. The US military also defended its operations following reports of civilian casualties and damage to infrastructure in Iran, stating adherence to the laws of armed conflict.

关注点 This event highlights the ongoing geopolitical tensions in the Strait of Hormuz, a critical chokepoint for global oil supply. The US military's assertion of control and facilitation of trade, contrasted with Iran's actions and claims, underscores the precarious security situation. The US's enforcement of a blockade and its claims of successful mine clearing suggest a proactive stance in maintaining maritime security, while the reported civilian casualties raise concerns about the conduct of military operations in the region.

Trump’s Latest Threats Loom Over Fed’s Interest Rate Decision

详细中文报道

美国联邦储备委员会（美联储）在三年多来首次宣布加息，将联邦基金利率目标区间上调至3.75%-4%，此前该利率区间为3.5%-3.75%。此次加息是美联储领导层一致通过的决定，尽管美国总统唐纳德·特朗普对此表示强烈反对，并此前呼吁降息。美联储主席凯文·沃什（Kevin Warsh）在会后新闻发布会上表示，此次加息是“负责任的”决定，因为“通胀过高且持续时间过长”。他指出，美国通胀已连续五年高于美联储2%的目标水平。 特朗普总统则在社交媒体上表示，利率“应该在1%或更低，因为我们是世界上信用最好的——远超他人”。他此前曾威胁，如果利率不被削减，将暂停广泛的贸易活动，并于周三重申了这一可能性。然而，美联储方面强调，尽管就业市场和整体经济表现强劲，但稳定物价仍是其首要任务。沃什承认美联储无法直接影响个别价格，如石油或食品价格，但可以通过抑制价格上涨的普遍化来发挥作用。 加息通常会使贷款（包括抵押贷款和信用卡）变得更加昂贵，从而抑制消费和投资，但同时也能提高储蓄的回报。美联储认为，较低的通胀对经济中最弱势群体最为有利。尽管加息可能对经济增长构成压力，但美联储认为当前经济的韧性足以支撑这一举措。此次加息也发生在美伊战争导致油价飙升，进而推高许多商品和服务成本的背景下，使得可负担性成为美国选民关注的首要问题之一。

Detailed English brief

The US Federal Reserve has raised interest rates for the first time in over three years, increasing the target range to 3.75%-4% in a unanimous decision aimed at curbing persistent high inflation. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh described the move as "responsible" due to inflation remaining above the Fed's 2% target for over five years. This decision was made despite strong opposition from President Donald Trump, who had called for rate cuts and threatened to halt trade if his demands were not met. Trump argued that rates should be much lower, citing the US's strong credit. Higher interest rates make borrowing more expensive, potentially slowing economic growth, but can also improve returns on savings. The Fed acknowledged that while it cannot control individual prices like oil, it can work to prevent broader price increases. The rate hike occurs amidst rising energy costs and affordability concerns among American voters.

关注点 This event signifies a major shift in US monetary policy, moving towards tightening conditions to combat inflation. The direct confrontation between the Federal Reserve's independent decision-making and President Trump's political pressure highlights a significant tension in US economic governance. The Fed's commitment to price stability, even in the face of presidential opposition and potential economic slowdown, underscores the central bank's perceived mandate. The implications for borrowing costs, investment, and overall economic growth will be closely watched.
