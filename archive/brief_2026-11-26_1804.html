The project, codenamed "Starmind," aims to meet the growing demand for artificial intelligence (AI) computing power by processing data in orbit and sending results back to Earth, utilizing solar power for the satellites. FCC Chairman Brendan Carr stated the project "opens a bold new frontier for the computing infrastructure needed to power America's leadership and growth," calling it "American ingenuity." However, rival Amazon has criticized the plan as "a lofty ambition rather than a real plan," while US astronomers expressed concerns that it would "significantly undermine" the public's ability to study the night sky. SpaceX plans to launch its first Starmind satellite as early as next year, with deployment required to begin by November 2033 and reach 50% by November 2038. It remains unclear if the full million satellites will be launched or if SpaceX sought approval for a higher number.
