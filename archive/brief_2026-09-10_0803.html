The rise in the 10-year yield is a key economic indicator, potentially leading to higher borrowing costs for the government and possibly influencing other lending rates across the economy. Market participants will be observing if the Treasury adjusts its strategy in response to this market reaction.

关注点 The bond market's rejection of the Treasury's repurchase plan and the subsequent rise in the 10-year Treasury yield to a three-year high indicate a lack of confidence in the effectiveness of the Treasury's measures to lower borrowing costs.
