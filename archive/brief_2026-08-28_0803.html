Syrian President Ahmed al-Sharaa has appointed Mazloum Abdi, the former leader of the Kurdish-led Syrian Democratic Forces (SDF), as a presidential advisor. This appointment, formalized by Decree No. 164 on Thursday, comes just three days after Abdi announced the dissolution of the SDF and its integration into the Syrian military. Abdi is among several senior Kurdish figures appointed to state positions recently. Earlier this year, another commander was made assistant defense minister for the eastern region. While the advisory role is less prominent than the proposed deputy defense minister and governor of Hasakah province position offered in January, it signifies a significant political integration. The agreement to fold the SDF into the central government, initially reached in March 2025, saw limited progress before its year-end deadline, leading to clashes in January where state forces took control of cities like Raqqa and Deir Az Zor from the SDF. A subsequent ceasefire placed these areas under government control and committed the SDF to join the defense and interior ministries under a 14-point plan. However, the YPJ, the SDF's women's units, were reportedly told the army structure lacks provisions for women's units. In parallel with military integration, President al-Sharaa issued a decree in January recognizing Kurdish as a national language and restoring citizenship to Syrian Kurds, marking the first formal recognition of Kurdish rights since Syria's independence in 1946. The SDF's dissolution leaves Damascus with formal authority over nearly all Syrian territory, advancing al-Sharaa's central promise of reunification since the overthrow of former leader Bashar al-Assad in December 2024.

关注点 This appointment marks a significant step towards national reunification in Syria under President al-Sharaa's government. By integrating the SDF and appointing its former leader as an advisor, the government is consolidating control over territory and potentially easing ethnic tensions. The formal recognition of Kurdish rights, including language and citizenship, is a crucial development in addressing long-standing grievances and building a more inclusive Syrian state.

英国国防部因失误追讨退伍军人养老金，引发不满

Military veterans told they owe thousands of pounds over clerical error
BBC · 2026-08-28 07:06
详细中文报道

英国国防部（MoD）承认，由于国家保险（National Insurance）计算错误，导致超过1000名退伍军人被多付了养老金，目前正向他们追讨总计510万英镑的款项。这一失误已导致许多退伍军人的月度养老金被大幅削减，引发了广泛的不满和担忧。 皇家炮兵退伍军人雷·洛顿（Ray Lawton）便是其中一例。他被告知因过去六年的多付金额而欠款8000英镑。不久后，他的月度军人养老金从550英镑被削减至365英镑。洛顿表示，他无法一次性支付8000英镑，这给他的财务状况带来了巨大压力。他表示，一生都信任军队，却在退役后收到这样的信件，感到非常震惊和失望。 洛顿收到的信件来自代表国防部管理养老金计划的公司Equiniti。信中解释说，其系统中的一个错误导致他多年来被多付了款项。 据报道，英国各地有许多退伍军人收到了类似的信件，要求他们偿还他们事先不知情的欠款。军事养老金协会（Forces Pension Society）表示，他们已了解到许多养老金多付的案例，涉及金额高达数万英镑，甚至有少数退伍军人被告知欠款达六位数。 国防部证实，这些错误涉及国家保险调整、养老金分享令（PSO）、提前离境付款（EDP）和国家养老金年龄（SPA）等多个方面。国防部表示正在逐案调查这些问题。 在受国家保险错误影响的335名退伍军人中，国防部称已有三分之一的人全额偿还了债务，另有52人达成了还款计划。迄今为止，已追回304,000英镑。然而，许多退伍军人对债务的清晰度以及如何处理这些问题表示担忧，并呼吁国防部提供更多解释和支持。

Detailed English brief

The UK's Ministry of Defence (MoD) has acknowledged that a clerical error in National Insurance calculations has led to overpayments of pensions to more than 1,000 retired service personnel, who are now being pursued for a total of £5.1 million. This has resulted in significant financial hardship and distress for many veterans. Ray Lawton, a Royal Artillery veteran, was informed in July that he owed £8,000 due to six years of overpayments. Subsequently, his monthly military pension was reduced from £550 to £365. Lawton expressed his inability to repay such a large sum, highlighting the severe financial strain. He stated his lifelong trust in the army was shaken by this unexpected demand. The letters, sent by Equiniti, the company administering the pension scheme on behalf of the MoD, attribute the overpayments to a system error. Numerous veterans across the UK have reportedly received similar letters demanding repayment of debts they were unaware of. The Forces Pension Society has noted many cases of overpayments amounting to tens of thousands of pounds, with some veterans facing six-figure demands. The MoD confirmed errors related to National Insurance adjustments, Pension Sharing Orders (PSO), Early Departure Payments (EDP), and State Pension Age (SPA), and stated that investigations are being conducted on a case-by-case basis. Of the 335 veterans affected by National Insurance errors, the MoD reported that one-third have fully repaid their debts, and 52 have agreed repayment plans. So far, £304,000 has been recovered. However, many veterans are seeking greater clarity on the debts and how they will be managed, calling for more support and explanation from the MoD.

关注点 This situation highlights a significant administrative failure within the UK Ministry of Defence, causing considerable financial and emotional distress to military veterans who have served the country. The pursuit of these debts, stemming from an MoD error, raises questions about accountability, fairness, and the support provided to former service members. It underscores the need for robust systems to prevent such errors and for compassionate handling of repayment when they do occur.

美国邮政服务实施新的邮件投票限制，引发法律挑战

US Postal Service says mail-in ballot restrictions now in effect
Al Jazeera · 2026-08-28 04:38
详细中文报道

美国邮政服务（USPS）于周四宣布，新的邮件投票限制措施现已生效。这些措施是在美国最高法院裁定支持特朗普政府的提议后实施的，但与此同时，仍有新的法律挑战正在进行中。 这些新程序规定，USPS将根据美国各州的输入信息编制合格选民名单，并且只向名单上的个人投递邮寄选票。此举正值各州准备在11月3日美国中期选举前向选民寄出数百万张选票之际。 USPS在一份声明中表示：“政府随后获得了这些禁令的救济，这意味着禁令不再阻止该规定在2026年11月3日或任何更早的联邦选举中实施。” 然而，这些规定并非没有争议。周一，美国最高法院以6-3的投票结果支持了特朗普政府的提议，为新措施铺平了道路。但其他法律挑战仍在继续，包括周三由24个州在马萨诸塞州一家联邦法院提起的最新诉讼。这些州的州总检察长认为，这些措施违宪，并且将在选举前夕对各州施加“新的、繁重的邮件投票要求”，造成“重大、紧迫和不可挽回的损害”。 邮件投票一直是特朗普总统攻击的目标。他声称邮件投票容易滋生欺诈，尽管有强有力的证据反驳了这一点，并且他本人也曾使用过邮寄投票方式。特朗普总统一直声称，各州在核实邮寄选票的寄送者身份方面做得不够充分，并且投票登记人员符合公民身份等要求。 今年3月，特朗普签署了一项行政命令，要求其政府创建合格选民名单，并指示邮政服务部门仅向名单上的人员投递邮寄选票。 邮政服务部门根据该指令制定了处理邮寄选票的规定，但在民主党官员和投票权倡导团体在数十个州的联邦法院提起法律挑战后，暂停了其实施。 最高法院周一的裁决，尽管以6-3的保守派多数票做出，但仍面临着来自24个州的持续法律挑战，这些挑战指控这些新规侵犯了投票权，并可能影响选举结果。

Detailed English brief

The United States Postal Service (USPS) has begun implementing new restrictions on mail-in ballots, even as legal challenges persist. These measures, supported by the Trump administration, require the USPS to compile lists of eligible voters and deliver mail-in ballots only to individuals on those lists. This comes as states prepare to send out millions of ballots for the upcoming midterm elections on November 3. The USPS stated that injunctions preventing the implementation of these rules have been lifted, allowing them to take effect for the November elections. However, 24 states have filed a new lawsuit in federal court, arguing that the measures are unconstitutional and would impose "significant, imminent, and irreparable harm" by creating "new and burdensome mail voting requirements" shortly before the elections. Mail-in voting has been a frequent target for President Trump, who has alleged it breeds fraud, despite a lack of substantial evidence. He has expressed concerns about states adequately verifying the identities of those sending mail-in ballots and ensuring voters meet eligibility requirements, such as citizenship. While the Supreme Court cleared the way for these new rules on Monday with a 6-3 conservative majority, the ongoing legal battles highlight deep divisions over voting access and election integrity. The restrictions could potentially disenfranchise voters and complicate the electoral process, particularly in states with large numbers of mail-in voters.

关注点 The implementation of new restrictions on mail-in ballots by the USPS, despite ongoing legal challenges, raises significant concerns about voting access and election integrity in the upcoming US midterm elections. The measures, supported by the Trump administration, could disproportionately affect certain demographics and potentially influence election outcomes. The legal battles highlight the contentious nature of voting regulations and the ongoing debate over balancing election security with voter accessibility.
