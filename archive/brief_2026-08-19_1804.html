伊朗战争推高英国通胀并使非洲富豪受益

Jump in energy bills drives UK inflation to highest rate for four months
BBC、The New York Times · 2026-08-19 17:02
详细中文报道

英国七月通胀率升至2.9%，为四个月来最高点，主要原因是能源价格上涨。根据国家统计局（ONS）的数据，天然气价格上涨是主要推手，导致能源价格上限在七月上调。能源价格飙升与二月爆发的“美伊战争”有关，该冲突限制了全球石油供应。Chancellor John Healey表示，伊朗战争持续影响英国国内物价，但强调英国经济具有韧性。他提到政府已采取措施减轻民众负担，如削减电费增值税和设定公交票价上限。能源咨询公司Cornwall Insight预测，由于持续的能源供应不确定性，特别是霍尔木兹海峡的潜在中断，以及欧洲持续的干旱导致电力需求增加，预计从十月起家庭能源账单将上涨4%。 与此同时，非洲首富Aliko Dangote的炼油厂因全球石油供应紧张而受益。随着全球燃料供应因伊朗战争而趋紧，非洲大陆的燃料需求转向了Dangote的炼油厂。该炼油厂位于尼日利亚，旨在满足国内和区域的燃料需求，其运营的成功与全球地缘政治紧张局势和石油市场波动密切相关。文章指出，不仅非洲民众转向该炼油厂获取燃料，其他国家和地区也可能因全球供应短缺而寻求其产品。

Detailed English brief

UK inflation rose to 2.9% in July, the highest rate in four months, primarily driven by surging energy prices. According to the Office for National Statistics (ONS), gas prices saw their steepest rise in nearly four years, contributing to an increase in the energy price cap in July. These energy price surges are linked to the "US-Iran war" that began in February, which has restricted global oil supplies. Chancellor John Healey acknowledged the ongoing impact of the Iran war on UK prices but asserted the resilience of the British economy, highlighting government measures to alleviate household costs. Energy consultancy Cornwall Insight forecasts a 4% rise in household energy bills from October due to continued uncertainty over energy supplies, particularly potential disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz, and increased demand for power generation amid Europe's heatwave. Concurrently, Africa's richest man, Aliko Dangote, has seen his refinery benefit significantly from the global fuel supply crunch exacerbated by the Iran war. As worldwide fuel supplies tighten, demand in Africa has increasingly turned to Dangote's refinery. The refinery, located in Nigeria, aims to meet domestic and regional fuel needs, and its operational success is closely tied to global geopolitical tensions and oil market volatility. The report suggests that not only African consumers but potentially other regions facing supply shortages are looking to the refinery for fuel.

关注点 The Iran war's impact is global, affecting energy prices in the UK and creating opportunities for major energy producers elsewhere. This highlights the interconnectedness of geopolitical events and their economic consequences, influencing inflation, household costs, and business opportunities.

美国起诉17名伊朗人，指控其进行大规模网络盗窃

US charges 17 Iranians over 'massive' cyber theft campaign
BBC · 2026-08-19 17:48
详细中文报道

美国司法部宣布起诉17名伊朗公民，指控他们自2013年以来，策划并执行了一场“大规模、国家支持”的网络攻击活动，旨在窃取美国机构的数据。根据司法部的说法，这些被指控的黑客属于伊朗的Mabna Institute，他们攻击了144所美国大学和42家私营企业的计算机系统。在此次行动中，他们据称窃取了超过31太字节的学术数据和知识产权，价值约34亿美元。美国联邦调查局（FBI）助理局长James C Barnacle Jr称此次协调的网络攻击是“对我们国家安全的严重威胁”。司法部指出，许多攻击是代表伊朗伊斯兰革命卫队（IRGC）以及“其他伊朗政府和大学客户”进行的。Barnacle表示，该行动得到了IRGC的支持，反映了“针对美国机构和全球伙伴的更广泛、有组织的努力”。司法部称，Mabna Institute的目标是全球约10万名学者的账户，并成功侵入了144所美国大学和另外178所国际学术机构的8000个教授电子邮件账户。此次行动从2013年左右开始，至少持续到2017年12月。此外，司法部还悬赏1000万美元，征求有关抓获五名被指控的“雇佣黑客”的信息。美国纽约南区检察官Jamie McDonald强调，网络行动已“成为国家权力的核心工具”，并警告其对美国安全和经济的危险影响。除了学术机构，Mabna还被指控攻击并侵入了至少五个美国联邦和州政府机构的员工电子邮件账户，以及位于德国、意大利、瑞士、瑞典和英国的11家外国公司的账户。

Detailed English brief

The U.S. Department of Justice has charged 17 Iranian nationals for allegedly conducting a years-long "coordinated campaign" of cyber attacks aimed at stealing data from U.S. organizations. According to the DOJ, these alleged hackers, associated with Iran's Mabna Institute, targeted the computer systems of 144 American universities and 42 private sector firms since at least 2013. The group is accused of stealing over 31 terabytes of academic data and intellectual property, valued at approximately $3.4 billion, during this "massive" operation. FBI Assistant Director in Charge James C Barnacle Jr. described the coordinated cyber attacks as "a serious threat to our national security." The DOJ stated that many of the attacks were carried out on behalf of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) and "other Iranian government and university clients." Barnacle noted that the operation, backed by the IRGC, reflects a "broader, organized effort to target U.S. institutions and global partners." The DOJ reported that Mabna Institute targeted the accounts of approximately 100,000 academics worldwide, successfully compromising 8,000 professor email accounts at 144 U.S. universities and 178 international academic institutions. The campaign reportedly began around 2013 and continued until at least December 2017. The department also announced a $10 million reward for information leading to the location of five of the alleged "hackers-for-hire." U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York Jamie McDonald emphasized that cyber operations have "become a central instrument of national power," warning of their dangerous impact on U.S. security and the economy. In addition to academic institutions, Mabna is alleged to have targeted and compromised employee email accounts from at least five U.S. federal and state government agencies, as well as 11 foreign companies in Germany, Italy, Switzerland, Sweden, and the UK.

关注点 This indictment highlights the ongoing cyber warfare between the U.S. and Iran, demonstrating Iran's alleged use of cyber capabilities for espionage and intellectual property theft, with significant implications for national security, academic research, and international relations.
