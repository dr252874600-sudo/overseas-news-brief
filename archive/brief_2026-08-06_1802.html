Concurrently, the US-Israel war on Iran in the Middle East is significantly disrupting global travel patterns. Airlines are rerouting flights to avoid affected airspace, leading to longer and more expensive journeys, particularly for travel to Asia and Australia. This has prompted European holidaymakers to opt for closer, safer destinations within Europe, such as Italy, Spain, Greece, and Norway. Tourism operators are adapting to this shift, with a notable increase in demand for European travel. While travel advisories for the Middle East have been lifted, concerns over regional instability continue to affect travel to hubs like Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

关注点 The dual pressures of Ukraine's defense needs and the geopolitical instability in the Middle East are creating complex challenges for international security and global commerce, impacting military aid decisions and reshaping international travel and tourism.

苏丹战争威胁联合国教科文组织世界遗产梅罗埃金字塔

Sudan war threatens ancient Meroe pyramids, a UNESCO Heritage Site
Al Jazeera · 2026-08-06 17:28
详细中文报道

苏丹自2023年4月爆发的内战已持续三年多，对该国丰富的文化遗产造成了毁灭性打击。其中，位于首都喀土穆以北约200公里的梅罗埃金字塔群，作为联合国教科文组织认定的世界遗产地，正面临前所未有的威胁。梅罗埃是古埃及时期库施王国统治者的陵墓所在地，拥有超过200座建于公元前800年至公元350年间的狭窄、陡峭的金字塔。 据报道，持续的战乱导致当地安全局势极度恶化，使得所有对金字塔的保护和维护工作完全停滞。现场的守卫人员表示，风吹来的沙子越积越厚，甚至侵入到金字塔和神庙内部，杂草也疯长，加速了建筑的腐蚀。他们担忧“金字塔已经严重恶化，石块开始脱落”。 考古学家马哈茂德·苏莱曼（Mahmoud Suleiman）警告说，“遗址可能面临灾难，发生倒塌是可能的”。他指出，除了战乱带来的直接影响，气候变化、苏丹考古工作者队伍的分散以及外国考察团的停止，都加剧了遗址的“显著恶化”。 此外，盐分从积聚的沙子中释放出来，正在侵蚀金字塔“本身就脆弱”的石材，并破坏古老的铭文。联合国教科文组织此前已发出警告，自冲突开始以来，苏丹已有超过100处文化遗址遭到破坏，至少22座博物馆被抢劫或摧毁。梅罗埃金字塔的困境是苏丹文化遗产保护面临的严峻挑战的一个缩影。

Detailed English brief

Sudan's ongoing civil war, now in its fourth year, is posing a severe threat to the ancient Meroe pyramids, a UNESCO World Heritage Site located north of Khartoum. The escalating conflict has halted all preservation and maintenance work due to deteriorating security. Reports indicate that windblown sand accumulation, encroaching vegetation, and salt erosion are accelerating the decay of these fragile structures, with archaeologists warning of potential collapses. The site, home to over 200 pyramids built between 800 BC and 350 AD as tombs for the rulers of the Kingdom of Kush, is suffering from stones falling and inscriptions being eroded. This situation is exacerbated by climate change, the dispersal of Sudan's archaeological workforce, and the cessation of foreign missions. UNESCO has previously warned that over 100 cultural sites in Sudan have been damaged and at least 22 museums looted or destroyed since the war began in April 2023.

关注点 The potential destruction of the Meroe pyramids represents a significant loss of irreplaceable cultural heritage, highlighting the devastating impact of conflict on historical sites and the urgent need for international attention and protection measures.

Years of budget cuts and political repression have weakened the state's ability to effectively rescue citizens trapped in rubble following the tremors. The government's limited capacity has necessitated seeking international aid to address the disaster. This situation highlights the erosion of public service capabilities due to economic hardship and the impact of the political system on social stability and citizen welfare. The earthquakes serve as a stark indicator of the government's diminished capacity to independently manage domestic crises, increasing its reliance on external assistance.

关注点 The Venezuelan government's inadequate response to the earthquakes underscores the critical link between economic stability, political freedom, and effective governance, particularly in disaster preparedness and response, with significant implications for the well-being of its citizens.

Detailed English brief

Koeyli Jaluka, 49, a senior professional, has applied for 442 jobs with little response, often being told she is "too senior," which she interprets as a euphemism for being too old. These experiences suggest that AI in recruitment might perpetuate biases, posing new challenges for mid-career women and prompting some to alter their CVs to mitigate age-related perceptions.

关注点 This event highlights a growing concern about the potential for AI in recruitment to exacerbate existing gender and age biases in the workplace. The experiences of multiple women suggest that AI systems, even if not intentionally programmed with bias, may inadvertently reflect and amplify societal prejudices, leading to discrimination against older women seeking employment. This has significant implications for workforce diversity, economic equality, and the ethical development and deployment of AI technologies.
