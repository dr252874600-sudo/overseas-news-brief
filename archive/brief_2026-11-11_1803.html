Detailed English brief

For the full 2025-2026 fiscal year, Pakistan paid $203.8 million for Indian imports. Organic chemicals and pharmaceutical products constituted 96.2% of these imports, with significant increases in vaccines and blood products, suggesting continued economic ties in essential sectors despite political tensions.
