美国将叙利亚移出“支持恐怖主义国家”名单

US removes Syria from ‘state sponsor of terrorism’ list
Al Jazeera · 2026-08-25 06:32
详细中文报道

美国财政部周一（8月24日）宣布，已将叙利亚从其“支持恐怖主义国家”名单中移除。这一决定撤销了在被推翻的阿萨德政权统治期间对该国实施的、已存在数十年的指定。该声明证实了上个月首次披露的决定。此外，美国还撤销了对努斯拉阵线（al-Nusrah Front）——即现任总统艾哈迈德·沙拉阿（Ahmed al-Sharaa），又称“圣战解放组织”（Hayat Tahrir al-Sham, HTS）——的全球“恐怖”组织认定。此举是在今年早些时候宣布并经过国会审查后进行的，它消除了叙利亚重新融入国际社会和市场、吸引投资的关键障碍。这进一步表明了对沙拉阿的信心，他发起了被许多专家描述为旨在争取西方国家支持的魅力攻势，包括与美国总统特朗普和法国总统马克龙会晤。财政部长斯科特·贝森特（Scott Bessent）在一份声明中表示：“今天的行动将有助于促进对叙利亚的额外投资，以促进政治和经济稳定”，并补充说这“符合特朗普总统关于向叙利亚提供制裁救济的承诺”。继2025年6月与欧盟一起解除对叙利亚的制裁后，美国于7月宣布决定将叙利亚从其“恐怖主义”名单中除名，理由是沙拉阿为统一国家所做的努力。HTS曾是“基地”组织在叙利亚的官方分支，由沙拉阿领导。在他的领导下，该组织在2024年12月通过一次闪电攻势推翻了前总统巴沙尔·阿萨德。此前，将一个国家列为“支持恐怖主义国家”会带来外国援助、国防出口和金融交易方面的限制。因此，叙利亚从该名单中除名已在该国受到欢迎，特别是受到金融官员的欢迎。叙利亚中央银行行长萨夫瓦特·拉斯兰（Safwat Raslan）在X（前身为Twitter）上表示，这是“一项历史性举措，使该国恢复其在世界经济体系中的应有地位”。他补充说，银行“正在努力建设一个现代化且可靠的金融体系”。叙利亚财政部长穆罕默德·巴尔尼耶（Mohammed Barnieh）也对此表示赞赏。

Detailed English brief

The United States has removed Syria from its list of "State Sponsors of Terrorism," the US Treasury announced on Monday. This revocation rescinds a decades-old designation placed on the country during the rule of the ousted Assad regime. The announcement also withdraws the designation of al-Nusrah Front, formerly led by President Ahmed al-Sharaa (also known as Hayat Tahrir al-Sham or HTS), as a global "terrorist" organization. This move, initially revealed last month and pending congressional review, removes a significant impediment to investment in Syria as the country seeks to reintegrate into the international community and markets. It is seen as a further vote of confidence in al-Sharaa, who has engaged in diplomatic efforts with Western powers. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent stated the action "will help foster additional investment in Syria to promote political and economic stability." Syrian officials have welcomed the decision, with the central bank governor calling it a "historic step that restores the country to its natural place in the global economic system."

关注点 The US decision to remove Syria from the State Sponsors of Terrorism list is a significant shift in US foreign policy towards the war-torn nation. It could pave the way for increased international investment and economic aid, potentially influencing the country's political and economic trajectory under the leadership of Ahmed al-Sharaa. This move may also signal a broader recalibration of US engagement in the Middle East and its approach to counter-terrorism designations.
