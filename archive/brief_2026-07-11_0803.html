Al Jazeera · 2026-07-11 07:18
详细中文报道

Detailed English brief

Cuba experienced its second nationwide blackout in less than a week, with the state-owned utility Union Electrica de Cuba announcing the outage began on Friday afternoon. This event exacerbates the already strained power supply on the island. Since the administration of US President Donald Trump took office, Cuba's oil supply has been severely impacted, highlighting the aging infrastructure. The US has maintained a long-standing trade embargo on Cuba and has implemented measures to prevent other nations from supplying fuel to the island. The Union Electrica de Cuba stated the outage began at 4:30 PM local time. This is the fourth island-wide outage this year, with two others occurring in March. Cuba produces only 40% of its oil needs as of 2023, according to the International Energy Agency. The Trump administration has taken steps to isolate Cuba, including cutting off oil and financial aid from Venezuela and threatening tariffs on countries supplying fuel to the island. Only one Russian oil tanker has reached Cuba since March.

关注点 The recurring blackouts in Cuba, exacerbated by US sanctions and fuel blockades, highlight the severe impact of geopolitical tensions on the daily lives of ordinary citizens and the stability of a nation's infrastructure. It also underscores the vulnerability of countries heavily reliant on external resources and subject to international pressure.

Detailed English brief

Deforestation in Brazil's Amazon rainforest has fallen to its lowest level in a decade during the first half of 2026, with an estimated 1,295 square kilometers cleared, a 38% drop compared to the same period last year, according to Brazil's National Institute for Space Research (INPE). This marks a significant milestone in President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva's efforts to curb forest destruction, a key promise during his 2022 election campaign. Under his predecessor, Jair Bolsonaro, deforestation rates peaked around 2022. While Lula's administration is credited with this reduction, it has faced criticism for authorizing expanded oil exploration near the Amazon River mouth. Tensions also exist with the US administration under President Donald Trump, who has proposed tariffs on Brazil, citing unfair trade practices and illegal deforestation. Lula's government has used the latest deforestation data to counter these claims, reaffirming its commitment to ending illegal deforestation by 2030.

关注点 The significant reduction in Amazon deforestation under President Lula is a positive development for global climate efforts and demonstrates the potential for policy changes to impact environmental outcomes. However, the ongoing criticism regarding oil exploration and trade tensions with the US highlight the complex balancing act between economic development, environmental protection, and international relations.

Detailed English brief

The White House stated the move was to ensure election security, asserting the President's right to remove individuals not fully aligned with the task. The EAC, established in 2002, provides non-binding guidelines, certifies voting systems, and maintains national mail voter registration forms. With the dismissal of the two Democratic appointees, and the prior resignations/departures of the Republican appointees, the commission is now vacant. This action comes just months before the midterm elections and has raised concerns about potential presidential interference in the electoral process. The EAC had previously declined to implement a part of a March 2025 executive order from Trump that required proof of citizenship on the national mail voter registration form, a decision that was later blocked by a federal judge for exceeding presidential authority.

关注点 The dissolution of the Election Assistance Commission leaves a critical federal body vacant just before a major election, raising significant concerns about the integrity and administration of US elections. It also reflects ongoing political tensions surrounding electoral processes and presidential authority.

The need to navigate these restrictions has led to a degree of artistic suppression and homogenization, but it has also spurred some artists to explore more innovative and metaphorical forms of expression. This phenomenon of "silent art" captures the complex mindset and delicate political ecosystem within Russia under the shadow of war.

关注点 The situation in Moscow's cultural sphere highlights the profound impact of political conflict on artistic freedom and expression. The 1,000 km-wide typhoon has already caused landslides in the southern Philippines, killing at least 15 people, with search and rescue operations ongoing. Taiwan is bracing for significant impact, with the Central Weather Administration warning of up to 1 meter of rainfall in the north and east. The island's defense ministry has placed approximately 29,000 soldiers on standby for relief efforts. Bavi is expected to be the largest storm to hit Taiwan by size since 1987. Across the region, dozens of flights have been cancelled, schools have suspended classes, and residents are stocking up on supplies. Farmers are rushing to protect crops, and fishermen are securing their vessels. China has also warned of a "significant impact" as the typhoon could move northward after hitting Fujian province, potentially affecting Jiangsu and Anhui provinces.

关注点 This event highlights the increasing intensity of typhoons in East Asia and their devastating impact on coastal communities.
