美国政府与科技巨头就人工智能监管展开博弈

A.I. Is ‘Better Informed’ Than Doctors, Kennedy Tells Industry-Backed MAHA Summit
The New York Times · 2026-09-30 07:41
详细中文报道

美国总统特朗普于周二（9月29日）召集了包括Meta、OpenAI和微软在内的全球顶尖科技公司高管，就人工智能（AI）的监管问题进行讨论。会议的重点在于如何确保AI技术的安全发展，并防止其潜在的负面影响。然而，此次会议并未促成任何具有法律约束力的监管协议。取而代之的是，与会科技公司签署了一项自愿性协议，承诺将采取一系列安全措施，包括进行风险评估、审计以及由第三方对AI系统进行评估。此外，他们还同意采纳特朗普总统偏好的术语，将“人工智能”重新命名为“超级智能”。特朗普总统在会后表示，他认为这项协议具有“道义上的约束力”，并相信科技公司会互相监督。然而，此次会议的参与者中，包括一些高管和政府官员（如众议院议长约翰逊和副总统万斯）对强制性安全措施持保留态度，认为这可能会损害美国的竞争力。 在同一天，卫生部长小肯尼迪（Robert F. Kennedy Jr.）在另一场由企业赞助的“让美国再次健康”峰会上发表了引人注目的言论。他与副总统万斯进行了一场“炉边谈话”，期间他高度赞扬了人工智能在医疗领域的潜力。小肯尼迪表示，AI可以为患者提供“比国内任何医生都更信息充分的第二意见”，并有能力“将我们从医疗暴政中解放出来”。他引用OpenAI首席执行官萨姆·奥特曼的话称，医生在没有咨询AI的情况下做出诊断或开处方是“医疗事故”。小肯尼迪还暗示，AI可能会挑战专家意见，例如在口罩、社交距离和疫苗有效性等问题上，AI可能会提供与官方建议不同的信息。卫生部长对AI的这种支持，正值特朗普政府面临要求加强AI监管的压力之际，而小肯尼迪的部门也因快速资助医疗领域的AI项目而受到批评。此次会议的赞助商包括与卫生部有业务往来的企业以及AI公司，这引发了对潜在利益冲突的担忧。目前尚不清楚这些自愿性协议的实际执行情况以及AI监管的未来走向。

Detailed English brief

US President Trump convened top executives from tech giants including Meta, OpenAI, and Microsoft to discuss Artificial Intelligence (AI) regulation. The meeting did not result in a binding regulatory agreement but yielded a voluntary safety policy accord, and the companies agreed to rebrand AI as "super intelligence." Trump stated the agreement was "morally binding" and that the companies would police themselves. However, some attendees, including Speaker Mike Johnson and Vice President JD Vance, believe formal safeguards could harm US competitiveness. Concurrently, Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. spoke at a corporate-sponsored "Make America Healthy Again" summit, praising AI's potential in healthcare. He suggested AI could offer a "much better informed" second opinion than doctors and "free us from medical tyranny." Citing OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, Kennedy implied doctors should consult AI for diagnoses and prescriptions, suggesting AI might challenge expert advice on issues like masks, social distancing, and vaccines. This endorsement comes amid pressure for AI regulation and criticism of his department's rapid funding of AI health projects. The summit's sponsors included companies with business before the Health Department and AI firms, raising concerns about potential conflicts of interest. The actual enforcement of voluntary agreements and the future direction of AI regulation remain uncertain.

关注点 The US government is navigating the complex landscape of AI regulation, with differing approaches from the executive branch and key agencies. President Trump's focus on voluntary measures and rebranding AI as "super intelligence" signals a preference for industry self-governance, potentially prioritizing innovation and economic competitiveness over stringent oversight. Meanwhile, Health Secretary Kennedy's strong advocacy for AI in healthcare, even suggesting it surpasses human doctors in some aspects, highlights a growing trend of integrating AI into critical sectors. This divergence in approaches raises questions about the effectiveness of current regulatory frameworks and the potential for AI to reshape public trust in both technology and expert institutions. The involvement of corporate sponsors in government-adjacent events also prompts scrutiny regarding influence and potential conflicts of interest in policy-making.

Detailed English brief

Ukraine's National Academy of Sciences headquarters suffered significant damage in a drone attack on Monday, with the building partially collapsing and catching fire, resulting in at least two deaths and several injuries. The academy is Ukraine's premier research institution, credited with over 90 percent of the country's civilian scientific discoveries in the past century. Its affiliated scientists have made advancements in astronomy and medicine, and crucially, have been instrumental in developing new military technologies, including drones and enhanced vehicle armor, particularly during the ongoing conflict. Scientists from Ukraine and internationally condemned the strike, with the academy stating, "Science has become a target... There can be no 'science is above politics' when science is under armed attacks." It remains unclear if the academy was intentionally targeted, though its proximity to a UKRtelecom data center, a frequent target of Russian attacks, is noted. Local residents view the attack as symbolic destruction, believing it will not extinguish the spirit of science. The strike represents a blow to Ukraine's scientific capabilities and raises concerns about the safety of research facilities during wartime.

关注点 The attack on Ukraine's National Academy of Sciences highlights the devastating impact of the ongoing conflict on critical scientific infrastructure and research capabilities. As a hub for innovation, particularly in military technology, the academy's damage could have long-term implications for Ukraine's defense and technological development. The incident also underscores the vulnerability of scientific institutions in wartime and raises international concerns about the deliberate targeting of research facilities, potentially hindering global scientific progress and collaboration.
