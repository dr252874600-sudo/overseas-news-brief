美伊战争持续，霍尔木兹海峡航运受阻，25名海员丧生

Trump to Meet With Putin and Xi in China, With Nuclear Proliferation on the Agenda
The New York Times、Al Jazeera · 2026-11-14 15:46
详细中文报道

联合国报告指出，自今年2月美伊战争爆发以来，霍尔木兹海峡及其周边地区的海上安全事件显著增加，截至11月11日，已有至少25名海员丧生。联合国助理秘书长凯莱德·希亚里在安理会简报会上表示，这种情况“不能成为新常态”，并呼吁“全面恢复该地区的海上航行”。他强调，10月份记录到的安全事件数量是自冲突开始以来的最高值，共有35起，而11月前11天已有10起。希亚里指出，“每天都有新的海上事件报告”，并强调“平民船只和商业航运不得成为目标”。伊朗方面则报告称，有三艘油轮在穿越霍尔木兹海峡“未经授权”或“限制区域”时触雷并起火。伊朗海军警告称，任何违规穿越霍尔木兹海峡东部限制区域的油轮都将“因触碰海军水雷而被点燃”。伊朗官员坚持认为，除非美国满足其要求，包括结束对伊朗港口的军事封锁、解除经济制裁、解冻伊朗资金、停止所有战线上的战斗以及明确宣布战争结束，否则海峡将不会开放。然而，美国军方声称已清理了海峡主要航道上的水雷，并表示该水道对商业航运开放。根据最新数据，通过霍尔木兹海峡的航运量比战前水平低约65%。

Detailed English brief

The Strait of Hormuz and its surrounding waters have seen a significant increase in maritime security incidents since the outbreak of the US-Iran war in February, with at least 25 seafarers killed as of November 11, according to a United Nations report. The UN Assistant Secretary-General for the Middle East, Asia and the Pacific, Khaled Khiari, stated during a Security Council briefing that the situation "cannot be allowed to become the new normal" and urged for the "full restoration of maritime navigation in the region." October recorded the highest number of incidents since the conflict began, with 35 events, and the first 11 days of November saw 10 incidents. Khiari emphasized that "civilian vessels and commercial shipping must not become targets." Iranian authorities reported that three oil tankers hit mines while transiting "unauthorized" or "restricted" areas of the Strait, catching fire. The Iranian navy warned that any offending tanker would be set ablaze. Iranian officials maintain the strait will not reopen until US demands are met, including an end to the naval blockade and sanctions. The US military claims the waterway is open for commercial shipping, though traffic is reportedly 65% below pre-war levels.

关注点 The escalating maritime incidents in the Strait of Hormuz, a critical global oil chokepoint, pose a significant threat to international trade and regional stability. The loss of life among seafarers and the disruption to shipping routes directly impact global energy markets and supply chains. The conflicting claims between Iran and the US regarding the safety of the strait highlight the volatile geopolitical situation and the potential for further escalation.

Detailed English brief

President Trump is scheduled to hold his first trilateral meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin during the upcoming Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit in China. The meeting's agenda will include discussions on the war against Iran and the global threat of nuclear proliferation. Notably, the war in Ukraine and a scandal involving Jensen Huang are not expected to be raised. White House spokeswoman Olivia Wales stated that the President "will discuss the reduction of nuclear weapons in the world, whose proliferation President Trump believes is very dangerous." This summit marks the third in-person meeting between Trump and Xi this year. US officials have expressed concerns about China's growing nuclear arsenal, while a US-Russia nuclear arms control treaty expired in February. Senior administration officials indicated that while the "difficult" topic of Beijing's nuclear arsenal will be addressed with Xi, no agreements on proliferation are anticipated, with the talks serving as a starting point. Elon Musk will accompany Trump to Shenzhen but will not participate in the trilateral meeting.

关注点 This trilateral meeting between the leaders of the world's three largest nuclear powers, amidst ongoing geopolitical tensions and concerns over nuclear proliferation, is highly significant. The discussions on nuclear weapons reduction and China's growing arsenal could shape future arms control efforts and great power relations. The inclusion of the Iran war as a topic also indicates a broader effort to address major global security challenges, though the exclusion of the Ukraine war suggests a focus on specific areas of potential agreement or strategic importance.

卡塔尔与土耳其签署互助防御条约，加强地区安全合作

Qatar and Turkiye sign a mutual defence pact: What do we know?
Al Jazeera · 2026-11-14 17:19
详细中文报道

卡塔尔和土耳其签署了一项新的互助防御协议，承诺在任何一方遭受外部武装袭击时采取共同立场。该协议是在伊斯坦布尔举行的土耳其-卡塔尔最高战略委员会高级别会议后达成的，会议由土耳其总统埃尔多安和卡塔尔埃米尔谢赫·塔米姆·本·哈马德·阿勒萨尼共同主持。此举正值地区应对美伊战争的后续影响以及霍尔木兹海峡航运中断之际。根据联合公报，该协议旨在加强集体防御，促进地区安全，并建立一个共同立场，以应对针对两国或其中一国的外部武装袭击。声明称，该协议不仅反映了签署方维护其主权和领土完整的承诺，也体现了他们为建立一个更安全、更稳定的地区秩序做出贡献的共同愿景。两国同意就安全威胁和国防政策进行相互磋商，并同意扩大联合军事演习、防务产业合作和人员培训交流。公报并未明确规定何种情况可能触发该协议，或联合应对将采取何种形式。卡塔尔《半岛报》周六的一篇社论称，会议还达成了关于教育、消费者保护以及和平、和解与冲突解决的其他协议。卡塔尔大学国际事务系助理教授阿卜杜拉·班达尔·阿尔-埃塔比告诉半岛电视台，该协议的目标不是组建针对任何特定国家的联盟，而是提高潜在侵略的成本，从而阻止其发生。他指出，双方的合作已建立在长期的军事伙伴关系之上，包括土耳其的军事存在、联合训练和国防制造。他认为，基于双方安全理念的趋同以及对威胁来源及其应对方式的共同看法，这种伙伴关系正在迈向一个更高级的阶段。

Detailed English brief

Qatar and Turkiye have signed a new mutual defense agreement, committing to a common stance against any external armed attack on either nation. The pact, finalized following a high-level meeting of the Turkiye-Qatar Supreme Strategic Committee in Istanbul, comes amid the ongoing US-Iran war and disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz. Analysts suggest the agreement's primary aim is deterrence rather than forming an offensive alliance. According to a joint communique, the deal seeks to bolster collective defense, promote regional security, and establish a unified position against external aggression. It reflects the signatories' commitment to safeguarding their sovereignty and territorial integrity, as well as their shared vision for a more stable regional order. The two countries agreed to consult on security threats and defense policy, and to expand joint military exercises, defense industry collaboration, and personnel training exchanges. The communique does not specify triggers for the agreement or the form of a joint response. Cooperation is seen as moving to a more advanced level, based on converging security doctrines and a shared view of regional threats.

关注点 The mutual defense pact between Qatar and Turkiye strengthens security cooperation between two key regional players, particularly in the context of the volatile Middle East. The agreement enhances their collective defense capabilities and signals a coordinated approach to regional security challenges, potentially influencing the regional balance of power and deterring potential aggressors. The emphasis on deterrence aligns with the current geopolitical climate, where stability is paramount.

详细中文报道

匈牙利调查人员在一次腐败调查中，于森林和建筑中搜出近1950万美元的隐藏现金。此次调查涉及前总理欧尔班时期的前发展部长塞斯塔克·米克洛什（Miklós Seszták）。匈牙利国家资产追回与保护办公室（National Asset Recovery and Protection Office）周五晚间发布声明称，11月12日至13日期间，在布达佩斯及匈牙利其他三个地区进行了搜查。调查人员在农场建筑、仓库、一处住宅以及“一处隐蔽地点的高价值保险箱”中发现了匈牙利福林、欧元和美元现金，甚至在森林地下也发现了现金。该机构公布的视频显示，警官在夜间户外挖掘，并检查成堆的现金，随后这些现金被存入国库。财政部长卡尔曼·安德拉斯（András Kárpátty）周五在脸书上表示，12名国库雇员花了八个小时才数完这些现金。此次发现正值对塞斯塔克·米克洛什的调查之际，他被指控在2014年至2018年担任发展部长期间，因与一家国有运输公司购买二手巴士及政府建筑项目相关的过高价格，涉嫌收受超过3720万美元的贿赂和回扣。塞斯塔克·米克洛什于9月被拘留，但他否认所有不当行为。今年4月结束了欧尔班16年执政的亲欧洲蒂萨党（Tisza Party）总理彼得·马扎尔（Péter Magyar）周五在脸书上表示，此次追回赃款标志着他所谓的“青年党-基督教民主人民党黑手党”的终结。青年党（Fidesz）是欧尔班的右翼政党，基督教民主人民党（KDNP）是其较小的执政盟友。欧尔班此前曾指责新政府利用调查迫使他退出政坛。其他前部长以及匈牙利央行前行长捷尔吉·马托尔奇（György Matolcsy）也因另案被捕。马托尔奇的案件涉及央行基金会涉嫌滥用公共资产，他本人否认有不当行为。匈牙利新闻网站HVG本周公布的一项民意调查显示，70%的受访者认为应该调查欧尔班时期的腐败嫌疑并追究责任人。

Detailed English brief

Hungarian investigators have uncovered nearly $19.5 million in hidden cash, including funds dug up from forests, as part of a corruption probe involving a former minister under ex-Prime Minister Viktor Orbán. The National Asset Recovery and Protection Office announced that searches conducted on November 12-13 in Budapest and three other locations yielded Hungarian forints, euros, and US dollars found in farm buildings, warehouses, a home, and a "high-value safe in a secluded location," as well as buried in forests. The discovery is linked to an investigation into Miklós Seszták, who served as development minister from 2014 to 2018 and is suspected of receiving over $37.2 million in bribes and kickbacks related to overpriced secondhand bus purchases by a state transport company and government construction projects. Seszták denies wrongdoing and was detained in September. Prime Minister Péter Magyar, whose party ended Orbán's long tenure, hailed the recovery as a blow against alleged corruption during the previous administration. The probe is part of a broader effort by the new government to investigate and hold accountable officials from Orbán's era, with other former ministers and the former central bank governor also facing separate investigations.

关注点 This significant discovery of hidden cash and the ongoing investigation into a former minister highlight the new Hungarian government's commitment to tackling alleged corruption from the previous administration. The scale of the hidden assets suggests substantial illicit financial activity, and the probe could lead to further accountability measures and reforms, impacting Hungary's political landscape and its relationship with the European Union, which has previously raised concerns about rule of law and corruption in Hungary.
