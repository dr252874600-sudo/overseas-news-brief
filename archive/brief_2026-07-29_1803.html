Accusations of AI-powered surveillance being used to track protesters have also emerged. The movement has not only led to a ministerial resignation but has also accelerated legislative action and potentially altered political communication styles.

The Bundeswehr aims to increase its active-duty force from the current approximately 180,000 to 260,000, along with a reserve force of 200,000. This move is part of Germany's broader strategy to bolster its defense capabilities in response to escalating security challenges and geopolitical shifts, particularly in light of the conflict in Ukraine. The potential return of conscription, suspended in 2011 in favor of an all-volunteer force, is a complex and potentially contentious issue. The government is weighing the necessity, feasibility, and societal implications of such a decision. While some young individuals, like aspiring musician Rayhan Butt who joined the army band, express willingness to serve and defend the country, the overall recruitment challenges persist. The decision on whether to reinstate conscription and the specific timeline and framework for its implementation are pending.
