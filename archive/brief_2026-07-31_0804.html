美政府首次动用“恐怖分子移除法院”遣返阿富汗裔女子

Trump administration appeals to never-before-used court for deportation
Al Jazeera · 2026-07-31 07:14
详细中文报道

美国特朗普政府于周四（7月30日）首次动用了1996年设立但在此前三十年间从未启用的“外国人恐怖分子移除法院”（Alien Terrorist Removal Court），以寻求遣返一名居住在美国的阿富汗裔永久居民纳齐拉·哈吉·扎达（Nazira Haji Zada）。批评人士认为，此举是特朗普政府规避正当程序保护的又一方式。 代理司法部长托德·布兰奇（Todd Blanche）辩称，该法院对于审理一名“本不应出现在美国”的外国恐怖分子案件是必要的。美国政府指控47岁的扎达参与了一项据称将在2024年选举日实施的枪击阴谋。她的两个儿子陶希德（Tawhedi）和阿卜杜拉·哈吉·扎达（Abdullah Haji Zada）已于2024年10月被捕，当时他们试图从一名卧底特工处购买两支步枪和500发子弹。两人均已认罪，阿卜杜拉被判处15年监禁，陶希德等待判刑。联邦调查局（FBI）表示，在两人试图购买武器时挫败了该阴谋。 然而，纳齐拉·哈吉·扎达本人并未面临任何刑事指控。尽管司法部声称有“证据优势”指向纳齐拉，但政府律师在移除法院庭审中表示，案件包含无法向公众披露的机密信息。FBI在一份7月15日的备忘录中写道，“底层信息被列为机密，因为披露将使恐怖分子和恐怖组织能够规避预防或侦测措施，或会暴露FBI或其他美国情报界的来源”。 尽管如此，法院通常有处理机密信息的程序，而移除法院的一名法官对政府的论点表示怀疑。此案的特殊性在于，政府试图绕过常规刑事司法程序，利用一个从未被使用过的、且其合法性备受争议的法院来驱逐一名未被刑事定罪的居民，这引发了对程序正义和政府权力滥用的担忧。

Detailed English brief

The Trump administration has invoked the seldom-used Alien Terrorist Removal Court for the first time to seek the deportation of Nazira Haji Zada, an Afghan-born legal permanent resident of the United States. Established in 1996, the court has never been utilized in the three decades since its inception, and its legality is debated. Critics argue this move represents another attempt by the administration to circumvent due process protections. Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche asserted the court's necessity for adjudicating the case of a "terrorist alien" who "never should have been here." The government accuses the 47-year-old Zada of participating in an alleged shooting plot planned for Election Day 2024. Her sons, Tawhedi and Abdullah Haji Zada, were arrested in October 2024 for attempting to purchase firearms and ammunition from an undercover agent; both have pleaded guilty, with Abdullah sentenced to 15 years and Tawhedi awaiting sentencing. The FBI stated it thwarted the plot at the time of the attempted purchase. However, Nazira Haji Zada herself faces no criminal charges. While the Justice Department claims a "preponderance of the evidence" against her, government lawyers argued in court that the case involves classified information that cannot be publicly disclosed, citing concerns that disclosure would compromise counter-terrorism measures and reveal sources. A judge on the Alien Terrorist Removal Court reportedly expressed skepticism regarding the government's arguments, as courts typically have procedures for handling classified information. This case is notable for the government's attempt to use an untested and controversial legal mechanism to deport a resident not facing criminal charges, raising concerns about procedural fairness and governmental overreach.

关注点 This marks the first-ever use of the Alien Terrorist Removal Court, a controversial legal mechanism created after 9/11. The administration's attempt to deport an individual without criminal charges, relying on classified evidence, raises significant questions about due process, transparency, and the potential for abuse of such powers, setting a potentially significant precedent for future immigration and national security cases.

Detailed English brief

Hundreds of migrants have entered Spain's North African enclave of Ceuta from Morocco over the past week, overwhelming local resources, according to the territory's leader. Juan Jesús Vivas urged the Spanish government to intervene, and Interior Minister Fernando Grande-Marlaska is expected to visit Ceuta on Friday, July 31. Footage showed migrants swimming and using flotation devices to reach Ceuta on Thursday, July 30, while others breached the border fence. Ceuta, located on Morocco's northern coast, has long been a key destination for migrants attempting to reach Europe. Earlier this month, Spain's Supreme Court ruled that migrants intercepted at sea en route to Ceuta or Melilla cannot be summarily returned to Morocco. The Spanish Interior Ministry believes human trafficking networks are exploiting this ruling to encourage migrant flows, primarily of young people. Spain maintains cooperation with Morocco on migration issues and has agreed to enhance efforts, including the prompt handover of all individuals who entered Ceuta illegally. Reports on the scale of the influx vary. Reuters cited Spanish media estimates of 2,000 to 3,000 people entering Ceuta on Thursday, though this figure remains unconfirmed by the Guardia Civil. Conversely, Spanish public broadcaster RTVE reported "hundreds" crossing during the day and noted that over 1,500, mostly young Moroccan migrants, had arrived in Ceuta over the past two weeks. Vivas stated that 60 bodies have been recovered at sea in recent months and warned of a humanitarian and security emergency, with reception centers for unaccompanied minors operating significantly over capacity.

关注点 This surge in migration to Ceuta highlights the ongoing challenges of irregular migration routes into Europe and the strain on border territories. The Spanish government's response, including the Interior Minister's visit and cooperation with Morocco, indicates the issue's high priority. The exploitation of a recent court ruling by trafficking networks underscores the complex interplay between legal decisions, border security, and humanitarian concerns.
