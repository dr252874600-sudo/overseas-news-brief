Detailed English brief

The UK government borrowed more than expected in July, with the deficit reaching £1.8 billion, significantly exceeding the Office for National Statistics' (ONS) forecast of a £500 million surplus. This means the government borrowed £2.3 billion more than predicted, potentially limiting the fiscal space for Chancellor John Healey as he prepares his first Budget on October 27. Economists warn that the higher-than-expected borrowing figure restricts Healey's ability to implement measures aimed at easing the cost of living. Healey has committed to "strong fiscal discipline" and adhering to his predecessor's fiscal rules, which aim to fund day-to-day spending through tax receipts by the end of the decade. While July's borrowing was substantially lower than June's £16 billion, boosted by self-assessed income tax receipts, experts note that this is a typical seasonal uplift. The ONS also reported that welfare spending, including benefits and state pensions, was £2 billion higher than the same period last year, contributing to the increased deficit. For the first four months of the fiscal year (April to July), borrowing totaled £56.7 billion, which is lower than last year but £2.3 billion above forecasts from the Office for Budget Responsibility (OBR).

关注点 This report indicates potential fiscal challenges for the UK government, as higher-than-expected borrowing could constrain its ability to fund key economic policies, particularly those aimed at alleviating the cost of living crisis. The figures suggest that government spending, particularly on welfare, is increasing, putting pressure on public finances. The Chancellor's commitment to fiscal discipline, while reassuring for long-term stability, may lead to difficult choices in the upcoming Budget, potentially impacting public services or the pace of economic stimulus.

The AFP alleges that the man enlisted in the Ukrainian military in May 2025, where he "gained access to information relating to their military personnel, units and locations." The investigation, a joint effort by the AFP and the Australian Security Intelligence Organisation (ASIO), is ongoing, with authorities executing search warrants and not ruling out further arrests. Russia's embassy in Australia stated it had no knowledge of the case.

关注点 This case highlights the ongoing global efforts to combat foreign interference and espionage, particularly in the context of the conflict in Ukraine. Australia's proactive investigation and charges demonstrate its commitment to national security and its zero-tolerance policy towards such activities. The incident underscores the complex geopolitical landscape, where individuals can be drawn into intelligence operations, and the potential risks involved in handling sensitive military information. 香港法院裁定，李卓人（69岁）和邹幸彤（41岁）两名活动人士因“煽动他人以非法手段组织、策划、实施或参与颠覆国家政权的行为”而被判有罪。此罪名依据的是中国引入的香港国安法，最高可判处10年监禁。另一名被告何俊仁（74岁）已于1月认罪。 李卓人和邹幸彤曾是已解散的“香港市民支援爱国民主运动联合会”（支联会）的领导成员。支联会成立于1989年5月，旨在支持北京天安门广场的民主运动。在接下来的三十多年里，支联会每年都在香港维多利亚公园组织烛光晚会，纪念1989年中国政府镇压民主抗议的事件，并呼吁中国政府为镇压负责。 自2020年以来，香港当局以新冠疫情为由禁止了这些集会，且集会至今未恢复。同年，香港国安法正式生效，该法律将更广泛的异见行为定为非法。在中国大陆，当局甚至禁止对1989年镇压事件的任何模糊提及。 邹幸彤在庭审中曾表示，国安法本身才是审判的对象。判决宣布时，邹幸彤在法庭上露出微笑，李卓人则情绪平静，向旁听席做出心形手势并双手合十表示感谢。 此次判决是香港国安法实施以来，针对纪念六四事件的又一次重大法律行动，进一步压缩了香港的言论和集会自由空间。判决的具体刑期将在稍后宣布。

Detailed English brief

Two Hong Kong activists, Lee Cheuk-wan (69) and Chow Hang-tung (41), have been found guilty of "inciting others to subvert state power" under the national security law. They were convicted for organizing annual candlelight vigils commemorating the 1989 Tiananmen Square crackdown. The law, introduced by Beijing, carries a maximum sentence of 10 years imprisonment. A third defendant, Albert Ho (74), had previously pleaded guilty in January. Lee and Chow were leaders of the now-defunct Hong Kong Alliance (HKA), which supported pro-democracy movements and annually organized vigils in Victoria Park. For decades, Hong Kong was one of the few places in Chinese territory where such commemorations were permitted. However, authorities banned these gatherings in 2020, citing COVID-19, and they have not resumed since the national security law came into effect that year. Mainland China has long banned any mention of the 1989 crackdown. Chow had stated in court that the law itself was on trial. During the verdict, Chow smiled while Lee showed little emotion. The sentencing is expected at a later date. This verdict represents a significant legal action against Tiananmen commemoration in Hong Kong, further restricting freedom of expression and assembly in the city.

关注点 This verdict underscores the continued erosion of freedoms in Hong Kong under the national security law, specifically targeting historical commemorations that are suppressed on the mainland.
