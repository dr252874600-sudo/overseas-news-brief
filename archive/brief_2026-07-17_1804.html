Before dissolving, the parliament passed legislation confirming the election date and approved controversial laws, including a communications law that centralizes broadcast oversight under government control and a bill shielding ultra-Orthodox men from military conscription, aimed at solidifying the coalition's base. 关注点 The dissolution of the Knesset and upcoming elections signal a period of significant political uncertainty in Israel. 关注点 This initiative by Trump Media represents a novel monetization strategy that directly links political influence with financial market data. By offering expedited access to potentially market-moving posts, the company aims to capitalize on the unique impact of Trump's public statements, creating a new revenue stream and potentially setting a precedent for how political figures' online communications are leveraged commercially.

The Guardian、BBC、The New York Times · 2026-07-17 17:57
详细中文报道

英国政府于2026年7月17日宣布，已根据紧急权力将英国最后一家原生钢生产商英国钢铁公司（British Steel）收归国有。此举旨在避免该国失去生产原生钢的能力，原生钢因其较低的缺陷率，被广泛用于铁路和大型建筑项目。英国政府认为，失去这一关键产业将对国家经济安全构成风险，因为英国将成为G7集团中唯一一个无法生产原生钢的国家。 英国钢铁公司位于斯肯索普的工厂是其主要的生产基地，雇佣了约2700名员工，占公司总劳动力的四分之三。该公司此前由中国京生集团（Jingye Group）于2020年收购。然而，在面临严峻的市场环境、关税以及向低碳生产技术转型的高昂成本后，京生集团于2025年3月表示，该工厂每日亏损约70万英镑，并启动了关闭工厂的程序。 英国国家审计署（National Audit Office）在2026年3月的一份报告中指出，斯肯索普钢厂的运营成本已上升至每日约130万英镑。英国政府此次接管，是为了确保工厂能够继续获得必要的供应以维持生产。 中国商务部对此表示强烈不满，称“坚决反对和强烈不满英国政府的决定”，并认为该决定“令人费解”。京生集团也正在寻求对国有化措施的赔偿。 英国钢铁生产已连续数十年下滑，该行业面临着巨大的财务压力。此次国有化标志着英国政府对关键工业进行干预，以维护国家经济安全和生产能力。

Detailed English brief

The UK government announced on July 17, 2026, that it has nationalized British Steel, the country's last producer of virgin steel, under emergency powers. This move aims to prevent the UK from losing its capacity to produce virgin steel, which is crucial for major construction projects like railways due to its low defect rate. The government views the loss of this capability as a risk to national economic security, as it would leave the UK as the only G7 nation unable to produce virgin steel. British Steel's Scunthorpe plant, employing approximately 2,700 people (three-quarters of its workforce), is the company's primary production site. The company was acquired by China's Jingye Group in 2020.
