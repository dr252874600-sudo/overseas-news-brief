Prosecutors stated this case reveals a state-sponsored operation targeting the company for its support of Ukraine, emphasizing that "we will not tolerate any efforts... to turn Silicon Valley into a base for foreign intelligence operations."

关注点 This arrest highlights ongoing foreign intelligence operations targeting critical technology sectors in the US, particularly those with ties to geopolitical conflicts like the war in Ukraine. Russian officials vowed to restore it "within a tight time frame."
