巴拉圭总统圣地亚哥·佩尼亚于11月5日宣布辞职，此前他因一家与其有关联的银行倒闭而引发的政治危机而面临巨大压力。Ueno银行进入破产程序，导致所有业务冻结，影响了约300万客户，相当于巴拉圭成年人口的约60%。许多民众无法访问他们的账户，包括养老金和部分公共资金，引发了街头抗议，要求佩尼亚辞职。 立法者指控佩尼亚，他直到去年还是该银行所有公司的一名股东，存在利益冲突和不当行为。佩尼亚否认存在不当行为，并表示他的辞职是为了保护国家的稳定、社会和平和民主制度。他的辞职发生在他所在的国会下议院投票支持启动对他的弹劾审判之后。 巴拉圭中央银行已接管了这家数字银行，国会也批准了向储户支付赔偿的程序。佩尼亚于2023年代表执政的保守派红党赢得选举。此次事件凸显了金融监管和潜在利益冲突在政治中的敏感性。

Detailed English brief

Paraguayan President Santiago Peña announced his resignation on November 5, following a political crisis triggered by the collapse of a bank linked to him. The insolvency of Ueno Bank, which froze all operations, affected approximately three million customers, representing about 60% of Paraguay's adult population. Many citizens were unable to access their accounts, including pensions and some public funds, leading to street protests demanding Peña's resignation. Lawmakers accused Peña, who was a shareholder in the bank's parent company until last year, of conflicts of interest and misconduct. Peña denied any impropriety, stating his resignation was to protect the nation's stability, social peace, and democratic institutions. His decision came after the lower house of Congress voted to pursue an impeachment trial against him. The Central Bank of Paraguay has seized the digital lender, and Congress has approved a process to compensate depositors. Peña was elected in 2023 representing the ruling conservative Colorado Party. The event highlights the sensitivity of financial regulation and potential conflicts of interest in politics.

关注点 The resignation of a head of state due to a banking crisis and impeachment proceedings is a significant political event with implications for national stability and public trust. It also raises questions about financial oversight and the accountability of political figures in relation to financial institutions.
