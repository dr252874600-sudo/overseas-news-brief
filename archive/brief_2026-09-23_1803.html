Key negotiation points include defining the scope of AI responsibilities, handling potential AI errors, and ensuring worker training and skill updates. Unions emphasize that AI adoption must be human-centric and should not compromise workers' rights. These negotiations represent early explorations of coexistence between labor and management in the AI era, foreshadowing potential structural changes and new challenges in the future labor market.

关注点 This event highlights a potential shift in how AI is perceived and discussed at the highest political levels, alongside the growing concerns and proactive measures being taken by labor unions to manage AI's impact on the workforce.
