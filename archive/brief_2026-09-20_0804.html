Detailed English brief

Herzog's office stated the decision was based on "the passage of time, the completion of his sentence, and the remorse he has expressed," following a request from Defense Minister Israel Katz. However, the move faced opposition from senior military officials, including Army Chief of Staff Lt. Gen.
