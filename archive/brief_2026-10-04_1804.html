太空资源争夺：月球所有权争议再起

Who Owns the Moon?
The New York Times · 2026-10-04 17:06
详细中文报道

1960年代签署的《外层空间条约》明确规定，外层空间，包括月球和其他天体，不得被国家据为己有，不得被占有，也不能被用于任何形式的永久性或临时性占有。这一原则旨在防止太空军事化和资源争夺，确保太空的和平利用。 然而，时至今日，随着科技的飞速发展和商业航天领域的蓬勃兴起，这一条约的约束力正面临前所未有的挑战。以埃隆·马斯克的SpaceX、杰夫·贝索斯的蓝色起源等为代表的私营航天公司，以及美国、中国等主要航天大国，都在积极推进月球探测和资源开发计划。 这些计划的背后，是巨大的商业利益驱动。月球上可能蕴藏着稀土、氦-3等宝贵资源，这些资源对于未来的能源供应和高科技产业至关重要。此外，月球还可以作为深空探测的中转站，以及未来太空旅游的目的地。 在这种背景下，虽然条约禁止国家主权宣示，但各方对太空资源的实际“控制权”和“使用权”的争夺已悄然展开。这可能通过建立太空基地、部署采矿设备、甚至通过技术和经济手段来影响对资源的获取和利用。 这种潜在的资源争夺引发了国际社会的广泛关注和担忧。如何在新时代背景下解释和执行《外层空间条约》，如何建立有效的国际合作机制，以避免太空资源的无序开发和潜在冲突，成为摆在国际社会面前的严峻课题。未来，围绕月球及其他太空资源的法律、伦理和地缘政治博弈将日益激烈。

Detailed English brief

The Outer Space Treaty of the 1960s explicitly states that outer space, including the Moon and other celestial bodies, cannot be claimed by any nation, nor can it be occupied or used for any form of permanent or temporary possession. This principle was established to prevent the militarization and resource competition in space, ensuring its peaceful exploration and use. However, in the present day, with rapid technological advancements and the burgeoning commercial space sector, the treaty's limitations are being challenged. Private space companies like Elon Musk's SpaceX and Jeff Bezos's Blue Origin, along with major spacefaring nations such as the United States and China, are actively pursuing lunar exploration and resource development initiatives. These endeavors are driven by significant commercial interests. The Moon is believed to hold valuable resources, including rare earth elements and Helium-3, which are crucial for future energy supplies and high-tech industries. Furthermore, the Moon could serve as a staging point for deep space missions and a destination for future space tourism. Against this backdrop, while the treaty prohibits national sovereignty claims, a silent competition for the actual 'control' and 'use rights' over space resources has begun. This competition may manifest through the establishment of space bases, deployment of mining equipment, or even through technological and economic means influencing resource acquisition and utilization. This potential resource competition has garnered widespread international attention and concern. How to interpret and enforce the Outer Space Treaty in this new era, and how to establish effective international cooperation mechanisms to avoid disorderly development and potential conflicts over space resources, are pressing issues facing the global community. The legal, ethical, and geopolitical contestation surrounding lunar and other space resources is expected to intensify in the future.

关注点 The renewed interest in lunar resources, despite existing international treaties, signals a potential shift in the geopolitical landscape of space exploration. The tension between the treaty's non-appropriation principle and the economic incentives for resource extraction could lead to new international disputes and necessitate a re-evaluation of space governance frameworks.
