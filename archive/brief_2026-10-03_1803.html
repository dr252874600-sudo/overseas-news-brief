Oil prices jumped over $4 a barrel on Thursday, and global diesel prices hit a record high of $6.50 per gallon last Friday. Trump suggested that the economy has a "PR" issue, implying that public perception might be worse than the reality. However, analysts point to persistent inflation and declining consumer purchasing power as significant challenges, despite slow job growth. The upcoming midterm elections could be influenced by these economic factors, with voter concerns about the economy potentially impacting the outcome.

Detailed English brief

The 24-year-old achieved his first title in nearly three years, finishing 18 under par and winning by three strokes. This victory exempts him from the typical 18 to 21 months of military service required for able-bodied South Korean males, with the exception of basic training lasting less than a month. South Korean law mandates military service for most men under the age of 28, but champions at the Asian Games and Olympic medalists are eligible for exemptions. Tottenham Hotspur player Son Heung-min previously received a similar exemption after winning the Asian Games in 2024, completing only three weeks of basic training. Kim traveled from the Presidents Cup in the United States to compete in the Asian Games, managing on minimal sleep and significant time difference. His career, which includes four PGA Tour titles and a Scottish Open victory in July, will now face less disruption. Another compatriot, SH Kim, missed out on an exemption as the South Korean team secured only a silver medal in the team event.

AI生成受害者陈述在美庭审中被判无效，引发对证据应用的讨论

US road rage killer's sentence quashed because AI video of victim was shown in court
BBC · 2026-10-03 07:06
详细中文报道

美国亚利桑那州的一起道路杀人案因AI生成证据的引入而出现戏剧性转折。被告Gabriel Horcasitas在2021年因枪杀37岁的受害者Christopher Pelkey被判处10年监禁。然而，Horcasitas的律师提出了上诉，核心论点是审判法官不应允许在量刑前播放由Pelkey家人使用AI技术制作的、模拟死者声音和形象的陈述视频。 亚利桑那州上诉法院于周三同意了这一上诉，裁定该AI视频的播放“越过了界限”。法院在判决书中明确指出：“该AI视频并非对事件的记录或对特定时刻的录制，而是基于受害者姐姐的想象，呈现了受害者及其想法的虚构画面。” 因此，法院判决Horcasitas必须接受重新量刑。 据此前报道，Pelkey的姐姐Stacey Wales去年曾向媒体表示，家人利用了死者的录音、视频和照片来重建他的形象，并制作了这段AI视频。视频中，AI版的Pelkey向杀害他的Gabriel Horcasitas说道：“我原谅你，我相信宽恕，也相信一位会宽恕的神。我一直都这样，现在依然如此。” 审理此案的法官Todd Lang在当时的量刑时，似乎对AI技术的运用表示赞赏，认为其传达了真诚的宽恕。 此次上诉法院的裁决，标志着AI生成内容在法律程序中的应用首次受到重大质疑。虽然AI技术在模拟和重建方面取得了显著进展，但其在法庭上作为证据或情感表达工具的有效性和合法性，引发了关于证据真实性、偏见以及对司法公正可能产生影响的广泛讨论。此案的判决结果可能为未来AI证据在司法实践中的应用设定新的先例。

Detailed English brief

An Arizona appeals court has overturned a sentence in a road rage killing case, ruling that the use of an AI-generated video statement from the deceased victim was improper. Gabriel Horcasitas was sentenced to 10 years for the 2021 murder of Christopher Pelkey. His legal team appealed, arguing that the AI video, created by Pelkey's family using voice recordings, videos, and pictures, should not have been shown in court.
