The conflict between the United States and Iran is escalating, with the U.S. implementing stricter measures to limit Iran's international activities. Amidst a seven-month war, U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced on September 22 that all Iranian airlines would be shut down through secondary sanctions, cutting off their access to the dollar system. This means any providers offering fuel, landing services, or ticketing to Iranian carriers would be excluded from the dollar system, effectively crippling Iran's aviation industry.
