Since regaining power in 2021, the Taliban have imposed strict controls on information dissemination and suppressed critical reporting, raising international concerns about human rights and press freedom.

关注点 The Taliban's suspension of BBC broadcasts in Afghanistan represents a significant blow to media freedom and access to independent information for Afghans.
