萨尔瓦多总统纳伊布·布克尔（Nayib Bukele）于周五正式注册参选，寻求在明年2月的选举中获得连任。这位民粹主义总统因其严厉打击暴力犯罪的政策而获得了极高的民众支持率，并被广泛预测将赢得连任。然而，他的连任之路引发了国内外对其执政方式的广泛批评。布克尔的政党“新思想”（Nuevas Ideas）利用其在国会中的超级多数地位，成功解除了总统任期限制，并将总统任期从五年延长至六年。批评者认为，布克尔通过削弱制衡机制来巩固权力，并指责他正在将萨尔瓦多变成一个“事实上的”一党制国家。人权观察组织（Human Rights Watch）的胡安·帕皮尔（Juan Pappier）指出，布克尔在“填补最高法院、按照自己的意愿重写宪法以及监禁或迫使批评者流亡”之后，正在寻求连续第三个任期。他认为，权力绝对集中是布克尔政府的标志性特征。布克尔本人则在竞选启动时表示，“萨尔瓦多拥有完全、彻底的开放民主”，并向世界传递信息，称“萨尔瓦多永远不会回到过去”。他还将自己的“模式”推广到海外，近期向海地派遣了约2500名士兵，以应对当地的帮派危机。然而，许多分析人士质疑布克尔的动机，认为他可能是在利用海地的危机来提升自己的国际声誉。值得注意的是，去年“新思想”党还利用其在国会的多数席位，取消了第二轮投票制，并将全国大选提前至2027年2月举行。

Detailed English brief

El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele has officially launched his campaign for re-election, a move that critics argue signals his intent to establish a de facto one-party state. Bukele, a populist leader, enjoys high approval ratings due to his aggressive crackdown on violent gangs, which has significantly reduced crime rates. However, his party, Nuevas Ideas, used its supermajority in Congress to remove presidential term limits and extend presidential terms from five to six years, leading to accusations of consolidating power and dismantling checks and balances. Human Rights Watch described Bukele's actions as packing the courts, rewriting the constitution, and silencing critics, enabling him to seek a third consecutive term. Bukele, however, maintains that El Salvador has a "total, complete open democracy" and is sending a message that the country will not return to its past. He has also begun exporting his security model, recently deploying troops to Haiti to combat gang violence, a move some analysts suggest is partly aimed at enhancing his international image. The consolidation of power under Bukele has raised significant concerns among international observers and human rights groups regarding the state of democracy in El Salvador.

德国年轻人抗议征兵制改革，担忧未来被军方决定

‘We don’t want our future decided by the military’: Young Germans protest
Al Jazeera · 2026-11-20 14:20
详细中文报道

德国各地爆发了年轻人的抗议活动，他们对政府的征兵制改革表示担忧和不满。今年1月1日，《军事服务现代化法案》生效后，德国国防军（Bundeswehr）启动了扩大规模的计划，目标是到2030年代中期将全职兵力增至约26万人，并增加20万预备役人员。作为这一计划的一部分，德国国防军向近60万名年轻人（包括男性、女性和其他性别）发送了兵役登记表。其中，男性必须填写强制性问题，而女性和非二元性别者则可以自愿填写。18岁的高中生菲尔·维林（Phil Werring）收到了兵役登记表，他表示只填写了强制性问题，因为他“不想回答所有问题”，并担心不完整填写会增加被征召的可能性。他认为，“我们不希望未来被军方决定”。此次征兵改革是德国自2022年俄罗斯全面入侵乌克兰以来，为加强国防而采取的一系列措施之一，还包括增加国防预算和加强情报部门的监控权力。德国国防部表示，这些努力正在取得积极成效，国防军已超出法定兵力增长范围。然而，此次改革引发了部分年轻人的担忧，他们担心国家安全局势的升级将导致个人自由受到更多限制，并对强制性兵役制度可能带来的影响感到不安。抗议活动表明，尽管政府认为有必要加强国防力量，但社会内部对于如何平衡国家安全需求与个人权利存在不同看法。

Detailed English brief

Young Germans are protesting nationwide against reforms to the conscription system, expressing concerns that their futures could be dictated by the military. Following the enactment of the Military Service Modernization Act on January 1, 2026, the German Armed Forces (Bundeswehr) have initiated an expansion plan aiming to increase full-time personnel to approximately 260,000 by the mid-2030s, alongside 200,000 reservists. As part of this drive, nearly 600,000 young individuals have received compulsory military registration forms. High school student Phil Werring, 18, stated he only answered the mandatory questions on the form, fearing that incomplete responses could increase his chances of being drafted, and articulated the sentiment, "We don't want our future decided by the military." This conscription reform is part of Germany's broader defense buildup since Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, which also includes increased defense budgets and surveillance powers. While the Ministry of Defence reports positive recruitment results, the reforms have sparked unease among some young people about potential restrictions on personal freedoms and the implications of mandatory service.

关注点 德国年轻人对征兵制改革的抗议，反映了社会对于国家安全需求与个人自由之间平衡的担忧，以及对军事化趋势的疑虑，可能影响德国国防政策的未来走向。

中国驻日本大使馆于11月19日发布公告，提醒在日中国公民，特别是中国籍登山者，切勿在非登山季（通常为每年7月初至9月初）攀登富士山。此举的背景是，年仅25岁的中国籍登山者陈杰于11月8日在富士山失联。其家属为鼓励搜救，悬赏50万元人民币（约合7.5万美元）寻找其生还者，若找到遗体则悬赏10万元人民币。这一高额悬赏引发了日本社会和中国国内部分人士的担忧，认为这可能刺激更多准备不足的登山者冒险尝试，从而增加发生意外的风险，甚至可能导致“次生灾害”。 富士山所在的山梨县知事宫崎县知事表示理解家属心情，但呼吁其不要做出可能危及他人的行为。此前，山梨县已宣布，从明年起，在非登山季需要直升机救援的登山者将被要求自行承担费用。此外，冬季登山者还需提交详细的登山计划。这些措施旨在让人们重新认识到冬季攀登富士山的危险性，并减少不必要的救援行动。 据报道，陈杰于11月8日与两名朋友一同出发，但因意识到山路关闭且装备不足，两名朋友在第五合目折返，陈杰则独自继续向上攀登。目前，搜救工作仍在进行中，但恶劣天气和潜在的危险给搜救带来了巨大挑战。此次事件凸显了在极端环境下进行户外活动所面临的风险，以及在信息传播和公众行为引导方面需要加强的方面。

Detailed English brief

The Chinese Embassy in Japan issued a warning on November 19th, urging Chinese citizens, particularly climbers, to refrain from ascending Mount Fuji during its off-season (typically early July to early September). This advisory comes after 25-year-old Chinese national Chen Jie went missing on the mountain on November 8th. Chen's family has offered a reward of 500,000 yuan (approximately $75,000) for his safe return, and 100,000 yuan for the recovery of his body. This substantial reward has raised concerns among some in Japan and China that it might encourage ill-prepared individuals to attempt risky climbs, potentially leading to more accidents and even secondary disasters. The governor of Yamanashi Prefecture, where Mount Fuji is located, acknowledged the family's distress but appealed for actions that do not endanger others. Yamanashi Prefecture had previously announced that helicopter rescue costs for off-season climbers would be borne by the climbers themselves starting next year, and that detailed climbing plans would be required for winter ascents. Chen had started his climb with two friends on November 8th, but they turned back due to closed trails and inadequate equipment. Chen continued alone from the fifth station. Search and rescue operations are ongoing amidst challenging weather conditions.

The family's reward, while well-intentioned, has sparked debate about encouraging risky behavior, underscoring the need for clear public safety messaging and responsible crisis response, especially in challenging environments like Mount Fuji during its closed season. It also reflects the growing trend of Chinese citizens traveling internationally for adventure activities, necessitating better pre-trip preparation and awareness of local regulations and risks.
