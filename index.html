Detailed English brief

美国中期选举历史回顾：总统党派常失利

How the president’s party has fared in every US midterm election since 1946
Al Jazeera · 2026-11-02 16:05
详细中文报道

美国中期选举，即总统任期过半时举行的选举，历来是选民对现任总统的一次“期中考”。自1946年以来，在20次中期选举中，有18次总统所属党派在众议院选举中丢失席位，平均损失26席。这种现象被称为“总统惩罚”或“中期选举惩罚”，通常被认为是选民利用中期选举来制衡白宫执政党。数据显示，民主党总统的党派在中期选举中平均损失约29个众议院席位，而共和党总统的党派平均损失约23席。历史上最大的席位损失发生在2010年，奥巴马执政期间民主党失去了64个众议院席位。共和党方面，最大损失发生在1958年，艾森豪威尔执政期间失去了50个席位。在20次中期选举中，有7次众议院的控制权发生了转移。参议院方面，总统党派在13次选举中丢失席位，但在6次选举中失去了参议院的控制权。本次中期选举（2026年）临近，民调显示，现任总统拜登所属的民主党可能难以打破这一历史规律，面临席位损失的风险。

Detailed English brief

Historical data from U.S. midterm elections since 1946 reveals a consistent trend of the president's party losing seats in the House of Representatives. In 18 out of 20 midterms, the incumbent party has seen its representation shrink, with an average loss of 26 seats. This phenomenon, known as the "presidential penalty" or "midterm penalty," is often interpreted as voters using the election to check the power of the party in the White House. Democratic presidents' parties have, on average, lost more seats (around 29) than Republican presidents' parties (around 23). The largest losses occurred in 2010 (Democrats, 64 seats) and 1958 (Republicans, 50 seats). Control of the House has changed hands in seven of these midterms. While the president's party has lost Senate seats in 13 midterms, control of the Senate has shifted in six instances. With the upcoming 2026 midterms, polls suggest the Democratic Party faces the traditional pressure of losing seats.

关注点 This analysis provides crucial historical context for understanding the potential outcomes of the upcoming U.S. midterm elections. The "presidential penalty" is a significant factor that often shapes the balance of power in Congress, impacting the president's ability to advance their agenda in the remaining years of their term. The data suggests a challenging electoral environment for the party currently holding the presidency.

Detailed English brief

An anthrax outbreak has killed at least 28 hippos in Virunga National Park in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC). The deaths, discovered over the past four weeks along the Semuliki River west of the park, have raised concerns among local communities, particularly amid the country's ongoing Ebola epidemic. Virunga National Park confirmed that the bacterium Bacillus anthracis is responsible for the wildlife deaths. Park officials warned that the disease can be transmitted to humans through contact with infected animals or by consuming their meat. Experts emphasize the significant ecological role of hippos and call for urgent containment measures. Virunga National Park is implementing surveillance, safe carcass management, and risk reduction strategies for wildlife and local populations. Public health advisories have been issued, urging residents to avoid contact with or consumption of dead animals and to report any carcasses promptly. Founded in 1925, Virunga is Africa's oldest national park and is renowned for its biodiversity.

关注点 This outbreak highlights the interconnectedness of wildlife health and human public health, especially in regions already facing significant health challenges like the DRC. The spread of anthrax poses a direct risk to local communities and underscores the importance of disease surveillance and rapid response in biodiversity hotspots like Virunga National Park.

Could Healthcare Become the Next Big Winner of the AI Revolution?

人工智能（AI）正迅速成为医疗保健行业变革的关键驱动力。从疾病的早期诊断到新药的研发，再到为患者量身定制的治疗方案，AI的应用正在不断拓展。例如，AI算法能够分析医学影像，其精度有时甚至能超越人类专家，从而实现更早、更准确的诊断。在药物研发领域，AI可以加速筛选潜在的候选药物，预测药物的有效性和副作用，大大缩短研发周期并降低成本。此外，AI还能通过分析大量的患者数据，识别疾病模式，预测疾病风险，并为患者提供个性化的健康管理建议。在医院运营方面，AI可以优化资源分配，预测患者流量，减少等待时间，提高整体运营效率。然而，AI在医疗保健领域的广泛应用也面临诸多挑战，包括敏感的患者数据隐私保护、严格的监管审批流程、以及将AI技术无缝集成到现有医疗体系中的复杂性。尽管存在这些障碍，但AI在提高诊断准确性、加速新疗法开发、降低医疗成本以及改善患者治疗效果方面的巨大潜力，使其有望成为医疗保健行业下一轮增长和创新的主要受益者。

Detailed English brief

Artificial intelligence (AI) is poised to significantly transform the healthcare industry, offering revolutionary advancements in diagnostics, drug discovery, personalized medicine, and operational efficiency. AI algorithms are demonstrating capabilities in medical image analysis that can match or exceed human accuracy, leading to earlier and more precise diagnoses. In pharmaceutical research, AI accelerates the identification of potential drug candidates and predicts their efficacy and side effects, thereby reducing development timelines and costs.
