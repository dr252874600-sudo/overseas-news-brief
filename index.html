下一步观察： 此次禁令的实施将对丹麦剩余的养貂场主产生直接影响。同时，这也反映了全球范围内对动物福利日益增长的关注，以及对传统产业进行可持续性评估的趋势。

Detailed English brief

Denmark was once the world's largest producer of mink pelts, but the industry has significantly declined due to global bans on fur farming, a drop in the fur trade, and a major Covid-19 outbreak in 2025 that led to the culling of millions of mink. The ban follows a two-year transition period. This move aligns with growing global concerns for animal welfare and signals a shift away from traditional industries perceived as ethically problematic.

关注点 Denmark's decision to ban mink farming reflects a broader global trend towards prioritizing animal welfare and sustainability over traditional industries with ethical concerns. This move could influence other countries' policies on fur farming and impact the global fur market, particularly given Denmark's historical significance in the industry.
