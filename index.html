Zelensky claimed that despite Trump's announcement of an "immediate" truce, Russia targeted "our gas stations, energy infrastructure, and other facilities," suggesting it was Putin's response to Trump. Ukraine's grid operator, Ukrenergo, confirmed new power outages in Kharkiv, Donetsk, and Zaporizhzhia due to damaged equipment and introduced electricity rationing. Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov, however, stated that Ukraine does not deserve a "respite" from a ceasefire.

关注点 This event highlights the ongoing conflict in Ukraine and the potential for de-escalation efforts to be undermined by continued military actions. President Trump's announcement of an energy ceasefire, if accurate, would represent a significant diplomatic development, but the reported Russian strikes cast doubt on its effectiveness and raise questions about the reliability of communication and adherence to agreements between the involved parties. Detailed English brief

The Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) stated that the drought, affecting about half the country, is compounding the impacts of conflict and displacement, reducing food availability, driving up prices, and affecting livelihoods. The IPC, comprising 21 organizations including UNICEF, the World Bank, WFP, and WHO, also noted extremely limited healthcare access and scarce clean water, leaving a malnourished population vulnerable to disease. Farmers' livelihoods have deteriorated, dependence on food aid is rising, and food insecurity, acute malnutrition, and excess mortality are expected to intensify. Poor vegetation growth and severe water shortages threaten livelihoods in several regions, with malnutrition rates exceeding famine thresholds in some areas. Separately, The New York Times reported on October 12 that dozens were killed in an airstrike at a remote gold mine near the Sudan-Egypt border, hundreds of miles from the civil war's front lines. Videos from the site appeared to show Egyptian munitions.

关注点 This report highlights a dual crisis in Sudan: a worsening humanitarian situation due to conflict and drought, with a significant risk of famine, and a concerning cross-border military incident. The IPC's warning indicates a severe and escalating food security crisis, potentially leading to widespread starvation and disease. The airstrike near the Egyptian border, if confirmed to involve Egyptian munitions, raises serious questions about regional stability, the involvement of external actors in the Sudanese conflict, and the potential for wider escalation. The combination of these events underscores the complex and dangerous dynamics unfolding in the region.

伊朗石油“中间商”欠款数十亿欧元，凸显制裁下的财政困境

Why do Iran’s oil ‘intermediaries’ owe the government billions?
Al Jazeera · 2026-10-13 05:16
详细中文报道

在国际社会对伊朗实施海军封锁和进一步制裁其石油出口之际，德黑兰正面临另一项严峻的财政问题：其先前石油销售的收入，据称已达数十亿欧元，至今未能收回。伊朗司法部发言人9月份表示，一个被称为“受托人”的石油中间商网络拖欠伊朗政府100亿至110亿欧元（约合110亿至120亿美元）。这一数额相当于伊朗本财政年度预计石油收入的一半。美国官员，包括财政部长斯科特·贝森特，将伊朗难以获得石油收入视为美国经济施压奏效的证据。今年8月，贝森特表示，“伊朗的影子银行体系”在美国针对该国的行动压力下“正在崩溃”。美国及其盟友以色列于2月下旬发动了对伊朗的战争。这一债务以及由此给伊朗政府造成的危机，源于德黑兰在2012年为应对美国制裁其石油出口而采取的石油销售安排。美国和欧洲自2012年起开始实施针对伊朗石油销售和收入收款的制裁，其公开理由是石油销售收入帮助资助了伊朗的核计划，限制伊朗石油出口可以阻止该计划的推进。作为回应，伊朗政府设计了一种规避国际社会对其石油销售制裁的方式。由于无法通过石油部并通过中央银行收款，伊朗开始向国内买家出售石油，或用石油偿还国家欠款机构的债务。这一安排催生了一个石油中间商网络，在伊朗常被称为“受托人网络”。这些中间商并非通过官方渠道出售所有石油，而是充当了伊朗政府与外国买家之间的桥梁，尤其是在制裁收紧的情况下。

Detailed English brief

Amidst an international naval blockade and further sanctions on its oil exports, Iran is grappling with a significant financial challenge: billions of euros in revenue from previous oil sales are allegedly owed to the government by a network of "intermediaries" or "trustees." A judiciary spokesperson stated in September that these intermediaries owe between 10 billion and 11 billion euros ($11-$12 billion), equivalent to half of Iran's projected oil revenue for the current fiscal year. U.S. officials, including Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, view Iran's struggles to access oil revenues as evidence of the effectiveness of U.S. economic pressure, noting that Iran's "shadow banking system" is buckling under the strain. This debt stems from an oil sales arrangement adopted in 2012 to circumvent U.S. sanctions targeting Iranian oil exports. Unable to sell oil through official channels and receive proceeds via the Central Bank due to sanctions imposed in 2012, Iran began selling oil to domestic buyers or using it to settle outstanding obligations. This led to the emergence of the "trustee network" of intermediaries who acted as a bridge between the Iranian government and foreign buyers, particularly under tightened sanctions.

关注点 This situation highlights the severe economic strain Iran is under due to international sanctions and the ongoing conflict initiated by the U.S. and Israel. The massive debt owed by oil intermediaries points to significant challenges in revenue collection, potentially impacting the government's ability to fund its operations and war efforts. It also reveals the complex and potentially opaque financial mechanisms Iran has employed to navigate sanctions, suggesting vulnerabilities within its economic system. The U.S. views this as a sign of success in its pressure campaign, indicating a potential strategic advantage in the ongoing geopolitical tensions.
