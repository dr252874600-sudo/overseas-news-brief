索马里最高法院裁定总统延任非法，加剧政治危机

Somalia's top court rejects president's term extension amid political crisis
BBC · 2026-11-06 16:18
详细中文报道

索马里最高法院于周四（11月5日）裁定，总统哈桑·谢赫·马哈茂德（Hassan Sheikh Mohamud）非法延长其任期，此举加剧了该国总统与司法部门之间的政治危机和对峙。此前，马哈茂德总统的四年任期已于今年5月届满，但其政府在3月通过议会批准的法律修改，将任期延长了一年。反对派指责总统利用宪法改革来延长其统治。 最高法院在周四的裁决中指出，宪法修改不能追溯适用于总统的当前任期。此外，法院还宣布总统此前解雇首席大法官巴什·优素福·艾哈迈德（Bashe Yusuf Ahmed）的决定“无效”，并指责总统府干预司法独立。总统府随后任命了易卜拉欣·伊德尔·苏莱曼（Ibrahim Idle Suleiman）接替艾哈迈德，并声称解雇是基于对司法行政的投诉，旨在实现公正透明的司法。然而，法院拒绝了这一解释，坚持认为首席大法官的罢免必须遵循既定的宪法程序。 法院还命令政治领导人在30天内就选举计划达成一致，并在90天内举行选举。这一裁决可能扰乱政府计划于明年举行的全国性选举。索马里自1969年以来未举行过全国性直接选举，三十多年来一直饱受内战困扰，伊斯兰极端组织“青年党”控制着许多农村地区。 前总统谢里夫·谢赫·艾哈迈德（Sharif Sheikh Ahmed）欢迎法院的裁决，称“国家正面临严重的政治危机”，并呼吁马哈茂德总统采取基于共识的方针。前总理哈西（Haad）也对局势升级表示担忧。目前尚不清楚总统府将如何回应法院的裁决，以及是否会遵守关于选举计划和时间的命令。此次事件凸显了索马里在重建国家机构和实现民主稳定方面面临的严峻挑战。

Detailed English brief

Somalia's Supreme Court ruled on Thursday that President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud unlawfully extended his term, escalating a political crisis and standoff between the presidency and the judiciary. The court also ordered political leaders to agree on an electoral plan within 30 days and hold elections within 90 days, potentially disrupting government plans for nationwide polls next year. Mohamud's four-year mandate expired in May, but his administration extended it by a year through law changes approved by parliament in March, a move opposition groups accuse him of using to prolong his rule. The court declared the president's dismissal of Chief Justice Bashe Yusuf Ahmed "null and void," accusing the presidency of interfering with judicial independence. The presidency had appointed Ibrahim Idle Suleiman to replace Ahmed, citing complaints about justice administration. However, the court insisted that the chief justice could only be removed through established constitutional procedures. Former President Sharif Sheikh Ahmed welcomed the ruling, calling it a "grave political crisis" and urging a consensus-based approach. The implications of the court's decision on the upcoming elections and the president's response remain uncertain, highlighting Somalia's challenges in achieving democratic stability.

关注点 The ruling by Somalia's Supreme Court directly challenges the executive branch's authority and could plunge the country into further political instability. The potential disruption of the electoral timeline, especially given Somalia's history of civil war and the ongoing threat from al-Shabaab, raises concerns about the country's path towards democratic governance and stability. The confrontation between the judiciary and the presidency underscores the fragility of state institutions and the challenges in establishing the rule of law.

英国首相敦促企业提前公布招聘广告薪资信息

Firms told to publish salaries in job adverts ahead of new law
BBC · 2026-11-06 15:36
详细中文报道

英国首相安迪·伯纳姆（Andy Burnham）近日向全国企业发出呼吁，敦促它们立即在招聘广告中公布薪资信息，而不是等待即将生效的强制性法律。政府计划尽快推进相关立法，使薪酬细节成为招聘广告的强制性内容。 政府方面表示，此举旨在帮助求职者，特别是那些初次进入就业市场的年轻人，并有助于解决和防止薪酬歧视问题。内阁办公厅在夏季进行的公众咨询显示，绝大多数（94%）受访者支持提高薪酬透明度。首相伯纳姆强调：“无论你是刚起步的年轻人，还是努力养家糊口的父母，在花费时间和精力申请工作之前，都应该知道这份工作的薪酬是多少。” 此项倡议被视为政府减少“待业、失业或接受培训”的年轻人（即“Neet”）数量努力的一部分，相关报告预计将在未来几周内发布。政府认为，提前公布薪资细节能够“揭示不合理的薪酬差异，增强工人挑战歧视的信心，并防止薪酬分歧升级为争议”。财政部金融秘书詹姆斯·默里（James Murray）也表示，薪酬“不应是猜测游戏”，并承认求职者在申请工作时可能感到的沮丧。 这些提议与欧盟即将推出的措施类似。欧盟的企业将被要求披露新职位的初始薪酬或薪酬范围，这些信息必须包含在发布的招聘广告中，或在面试前告知求职者。根据英国招聘网站Adzuna的数据，截至9月，其网站上51%的招聘广告已包含薪资信息。 然而，一些行业团体对强制性薪酬披露表示担忧，认为设定薪酬是一个复杂的过程，监管招聘广告并非总是合适。尽管如此，政府的立场是明确的，即希望通过提高透明度来促进更公平的就业市场。

Detailed English brief

UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham is urging businesses to publish salary information in job advertisements immediately, rather than waiting for a new mandatory law. The government plans to introduce legislation making pay details compulsory in adverts, arguing it will help jobseekers, particularly young people entering the workforce, and aid in preventing pay discrimination. This initiative aligns with similar measures being introduced in the European Union, where employers will be required to disclose pay or salary ranges for new roles. A government consultation found overwhelming support for increased pay transparency, with 94% of respondents backing the move. Prime Minister Burnham stated that individuals should know a job's pay before applying. The government believes upfront salary disclosure can expose unjustified pay gaps and empower workers to challenge discrimination. Financial Secretary to the Treasury James Murray called it a "common sense reform." While some industry groups express concerns about the complexity of setting salaries and regulating adverts, the government is pushing forward to foster a fairer employment market.

关注点 The UK government's push for mandatory salary disclosure in job adverts represents a significant policy shift aimed at increasing pay transparency and tackling pay discrimination. If successfully implemented, it could empower job seekers, particularly those from disadvantaged backgrounds, by providing crucial information upfront. This move, mirroring EU regulations, could set a precedent for other countries and potentially reshape hiring practices and wage negotiations within the UK labor market, impacting both employers and employees.
