Detailed English brief

The Russia-Ukraine war has inflicted devastating damage on the Black Sea's ecosystem, resulting in the deaths of over 120,000 dolphins and widespread pollution. According to Ivan Rusev, a researcher at Ukraine's Tuzly Lagoons National Park, the conflict has led to a significant increase in dolphin mortality, with war activities such as naval sonar, missile launches, and underwater explosions disorienting marine life and impairing their ability to hunt. This disorientation, coupled with starvation and subsequent infections, has been a major cause of death. Additionally, sunken warships and vessels have released substantial amounts of oil and chemical pollutants into the water, further degrading the already fragile Black Sea environment. The ecological damage is extensive, impacting the entire food chain and threatening the long-term health of the marine ecosystem.

关注点 The ecological devastation of the Black Sea due to the Russia-Ukraine war highlights the far-reaching consequences of armed conflict beyond immediate human casualties, impacting global biodiversity and environmental health.
