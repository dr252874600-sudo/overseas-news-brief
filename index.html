Detailed English brief

Helm had written on X (formerly Twitter) on October 15, "God help us authors who support Palestine and Palestine Action." The post was in response to author Sally Rooney's announcement that she would donate her royalties to Palestine Action, a group aiming to "end global participation in Israel's genocidal and apartheid regime." Consequently, Rooney's publisher, Faber, decided not to sell her books in the UK due to the organization's proscribed status. The Metropolitan Police assessed Helm's post and determined it "did not meet the criminal threshold," thus concluding "no further police action will be taken." Palestine Action was proscribed as a terrorist organization in the UK in July 2025, making support for it a criminal offense. The decision not to prosecute Helm has implications for discussions around free speech and the boundaries of counter-terrorism legislation.

关注点 This case touches upon the sensitive intersection of political expression, proscribed organizations, and potential legal ramifications in the UK.
