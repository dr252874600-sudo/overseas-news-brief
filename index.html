Detailed English brief

Transitional President Gen Horta N'Tam announced in a decree that the elections, due on December 6, will now take place on November 26, 2028. This announcement comes exactly one year after the military seized power, promising to return the country to civilian rule within 12 months. The presidency stated in a release that an assessment of electoral preparations found insufficient "technical, material and financial conditions" to hold the polls as planned. Gen N'Tam was sworn in as transitional president a day after the military overthrew President Umaro Sissoco Embaló in November 2025. Embaló, who has since been living in exile in Morocco, was barred from returning to Guinea-Bissau last month. Guinea-Bissau has experienced several coups and attempted coups since gaining independence in 1974.
