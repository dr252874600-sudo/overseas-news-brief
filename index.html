内塔尼亚胡腐败案关键指控或被撤销，以色列总理寻求政治生存

Judges in Israel Suggest Dropping Key Corruption Case Against Netanyahu
The New York Times、Al Jazeera · 2026-11-26 04:45
详细中文报道

以色列当地时间11月25日，耶路撒冷一家法院的法官在审理总理本雅明·内塔尼亚胡的腐败案时，建议撤销其中最严重的一项指控——涉及贿赂的“案件4000”。法官里夫卡·弗里德曼-费尔德曼表示，证明该项贿赂指控面临“相当大的困难”。该案指控内塔尼亚胡在担任总理期间，利用职权为以色列电信巨头贝泽克（Bezeq）提供监管上的便利，以换取其控股股东绍尔·埃洛维奇（Shaul Elovitch）旗下的Walla新闻网站给予他有利的报道。 内塔尼亚胡在特拉维夫发表电视讲话，对法官的建议表示欢迎，称其“捏造的案件”正在瓦解，并呼吁立即结束这场持续了十年的审判，称其给自己和家人带来了“可怕的痛苦”。他坚称自己无罪，并指责这些指控是出于政治动机的阴谋。 此案的进展正值内塔尼亚胡政治生涯的关键时刻。在10月27日以色列大选未能产生明确赢家后，他正为组建新政府而努力。他的主要竞争对手、国家团结党领导人甘茨（Benny Gantz）已被总统授权组建政府。如果内塔尼亚胡失去总理职位，他将失去免受潜在牢狱之灾的政治豁免权。 除了“案件4000”，法官还建议重新考虑另一项指控，并建议通过协议解决第三项指控。内塔尼亚胡是第一位面临审判的以色列在任总理。以色列总检察长加利·巴哈拉夫-米亚拉（Gali Baharav-Miara）已表示将考虑法官的建议。目前尚不清楚这些建议是否具有法律约束力，以及检方将如何回应。

Detailed English brief

Israeli judges on November 25 recommended dropping the bribery charge in Case 4000, the most serious of the corruption charges against Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, citing "considerable difficulties" in proving the case. Judge Rivka Friedman-Feldman stated that proving the bribery charge faced significant hurdles. The case alleges that Netanyahu, while prime minister, offered regulatory favors to the telecommunications giant Bezeq in exchange for favorable coverage from its news site Walla, owned by controlling shareholder Shaul Elovitch. Netanyahu welcomed the recommendation, declaring in a televised address that the "fabricated cases" against him were collapsing and called for an immediate end to the decade-long trial. He maintains his innocence and claims the charges are politically motivated. The development comes at a critical juncture for Netanyahu's political survival. Following inconclusive elections on October 27, he is vying to form a government. His main rival, Benny Gantz, has been tasked with forming a government. If Netanyahu loses his prime ministerial immunity, he could face imprisonment. In addition to Case 4000, judges also suggested reconsidering another charge and resolving a third through an agreement. Netanyahu is the first sitting Israeli prime minister to stand trial. Attorney General Gali Baharav-Miara stated she would consider the judges' recommendations. The legal implications and the prosecution's response remain to be seen.

关注点 The potential dropping of key charges in Prime Minister Netanyahu's corruption trial could significantly impact his political future and the ongoing political instability in Israel. It also raises questions about the integrity of the judicial process and the prosecution's ability to secure convictions in high-profile cases.

The New York Times · 2026-11-26 00:22
详细中文报道

该报告认为，拉马福萨可能违反了南非宪法中关于披露其私人利益的规定，并且可能滥用职权试图掩盖盗窃案的真相。报告还指出，总统可能在议会作证时误导了议员。 然而，南非高等法院在11月25日的裁决中，撤销了该报告中关于拉马福萨可能违反宪法的关键部分。法院认为，该报告在程序上存在缺陷，并且对证据的解读存在问题。法官裁定，该报告未能充分证明总统存在不当行为，因此不能作为弹劾总统的依据。 尽管法院的裁决为拉马福萨暂时解除了弹劾的直接威胁，但他仍面临其他与此案相关的调查和政治压力。反对党一直要求他辞职，并利用“农场门”事件对其施加压力。此次法院的裁决，虽然减轻了眼前的危机，但并未完全消除围绕总统的争议。 拉马福萨自2018年上任以来，一直致力于打击南非根深蒂固的腐败问题。然而，“农场门”事件的曝光，以及他处理此事的透明度问题，严重损害了他的声誉。此次法院的裁决，虽然在法律层面上为他提供了保护，但其政治影响仍有待观察。南非国民议会（Parliament）将需要根据法院的裁决，决定下一步的行动。目前尚不清楚议会是否会继续推进弹劾程序，或者是否会接受法院的裁决。 此次事件也凸显了南非政治体系中对总统权力的制约和监督机制。法院在其中扮演了关键角色，对总统的行为进行司法审查。然而，政治上的不确定性以及公众对腐败问题的担忧，仍然是南非面临的严峻挑战。

Detailed English brief

South African President Cyril Ramaphosa has received a reprieve in the "Farmgate" scandal, as a court on November 25 overturned a report that could have led to his impeachment. The report, issued by an independent panel, accused Ramaphosa of attempting to conceal the theft of over $580,000 in cash from his private Phala Phala game farm in 2020. The panel had alleged that Ramaphosa may have violated the constitution by failing to disclose his private interests and potentially abused his power to cover up the incident. It also suggested he may have misled Parliament during his testimony. However, the High Court ruled that the report's findings regarding Ramaphosa's alleged constitutional violations were flawed and based on an inadequate interpretation of evidence. The court found that the report did not sufficiently prove misconduct by the president, thus invalidating it as grounds for impeachment. While this ruling temporarily alleviates the immediate threat of impeachment, Ramaphosa still faces political pressure and other investigations related to the scandal. Opposition parties have called for his resignation, using the "Farmgate" incident to pressure him. The court's decision offers legal protection for now, but its long-term political impact remains uncertain. The case has damaged Ramaphosa's reputation, which has been built on a platform of fighting corruption since he took office in 2018. The court's intervention highlights the checks and balances within South Africa's political system, but public concerns about corruption persist.

关注点 The court's decision to overturn the impeachment report provides a temporary political lifeline for President Ramaphosa, allowing him to continue his anti-corruption agenda. However, the underlying allegations and the political fallout from the "Farmgate" scandal continue to cast a shadow over his presidency and South Africa's political stability, highlighting the ongoing challenges in combating corruption and maintaining public trust.

详细中文报道

朝鲜外交部宣布瑞士驻朝鲜外交办公室负责人为“不受欢迎的人”，并要求其在48小时内离境，同时要求关闭该办事处。此举是对瑞士近期对朝鲜松岛园国际儿童营实施制裁的回应。据报道，该儿童营位于元山市，有来自俄罗斯和中国等国的儿童参加，并被指控参与将来自俄罗斯占领的乌克兰地区的儿童转移。朝鲜国家通讯社朝中社称瑞士的制裁是“赤裸裸的敌对行为”。 此次外交驱逐发生在一个月前，瑞士外交部长伊尼亚齐奥·卡西斯刚刚罕见地访问了朝鲜，并正式重新开放了瑞士在平壤的办事处，讨论正常化关系。瑞士当局表示，两国关系将通过朝鲜驻伯尔尼大使馆和瑞士驻北京大使馆维持。 瑞士与朝鲜的外交关系始于1974年，1997年瑞士在平壤设立了合作办公室。该办公室在新冠疫情期间关闭，并已关闭六年。 瑞士外交部强调，该国“区分其制裁政策与其维持与所有国家对话的承诺”。并补充说，“重新开放平壤办事处丝毫未影响瑞士执行与朝鲜核计划相关的联合国制裁，也未影响其执行欧盟针对朝鲜在俄罗斯战争背景下实施的制裁”。 朝鲜近期加强了与俄罗斯的联系，据报道，数千名朝鲜士兵已被部署以支持俄罗斯在乌克兰的战争。此前，瑞士外交部长卡西斯在10月29日访问朝鲜时，曾与朝鲜官员讨论了双边关系正常化的问题。

Detailed English brief

North Korea has declared the head of the Swiss diplomatic mission in Pyongyang persona non grata and demanded the closure of the office within 48 hours. This action follows Switzerland's imposition of sanctions on the Songdowon International Children's Camp in Wonsan, which reportedly hosts children from various nationalities, including Russian and Chinese, and is accused of involvement in transferring children from Russian-occupied areas of Ukraine. North Korea's state news agency KCNA denounced the sanctions as a "blatant hostile act." The expulsion comes weeks after Swiss Foreign Minister Ignazio Cassis made a rare visit to North Korea, during which he officially reopened the Swiss cooperation office in Pyongyang and discussed normalizing relations. Swiss authorities stated that bilateral relations would be maintained through their respective embassies in Bern and Beijing. Switzerland, which established diplomatic ties with North Korea in 1974, reopened its cooperation office in Pyongyang in 1997. The office had been closed during the COVID-19 pandemic for six years. The Swiss Department of Foreign Affairs emphasized that the country "distinguishes between its sanctions policy and its commitment to maintaining dialogue with all countries." They clarified that the reopening of the Pyongyang office "did not in any way affect Switzerland's implementation of UN sanctions related to North Korea's nuclear program, nor its implementation of EU sanctions targeting North Korea in the context of Russia's war against Ukraine." North Korea has recently strengthened ties with Russia, with thousands of its soldiers reportedly deployed to support Russia's war in Ukraine. Cassis's visit on October 29 aimed to discuss the normalization of bilateral relations.

关注点 This event highlights the complex diplomatic balancing act Switzerland is attempting in its relations with North Korea, particularly in the context of international sanctions and geopolitical tensions surrounding Russia's war in Ukraine. North Korea's retaliatory expulsion demonstrates its sensitivity to sanctions and its willingness to use diplomatic pressure against countries that implement them, even those that maintain dialogue.
