Restored wells bring life back to Hebron’s occupied Old City

详细中文报道

在以色列占领和军事封锁的持续压力下，约旦河西岸希伯伦老城正经历严重的水资源短缺，给当地巴勒斯坦居民的日常生活带来了巨大挑战。许多家庭难以获得充足的饮用水，简单的家务也因此变得困难重重。由于狭窄的街道和众多的检查站，运水车难以进入，而市政供水每月仅能到达一次，居民也缺乏足够的储水能力。 为了应对这一危机，希伯伦重建委员会于去年7月启动了一项修复被遗弃古井的项目。该项目邀请老城区居民登记其房产内的废弃水井。项目启动初期，超过70户家庭进行了登记，至今已有约215户家庭注册。委员会目前已修复了108口古井，并计划在明年完成剩余古井的修复工作。 该项目旨在实现三个相互关联的目标：一是将这些古井作为老城遗产和建筑肌理的一部分加以保护；二是改善居民获取清洁水源的途径；三是在以色列定居者和当局施加巨大压力、试图迫使居民离开的情况下，增强居民留在其家园和社区的能力。委员会总干事穆罕纳德·贾巴里表示，“找到水源意味着为老城带来生机”。该项目不仅解决了实际的水源问题，也承载着保护文化遗产和维护居民生存权的意义。

Detailed English brief

In the occupied Old City of Hebron, West Bank, severe water shortages, exacerbated by Israeli occupation and military blockades, are significantly impacting Palestinian residents' daily lives. Many families struggle to access sufficient water for basic needs, with municipal supplies arriving only once a month and limited storage capacity. Water tankers face difficulties reaching homes due to narrow streets and checkpoints. To address this crisis, the Hebron Rehabilitation Committee launched a project in July last year to restore abandoned wells. Residents were invited to register neglected wells on their properties. Over 70 households registered initially, with around 215 signing up since. The committee has so far restored 108 wells and aims to complete the remaining work next year. The project has three interconnected goals: preserving the wells as part of the Old City's heritage, improving access to clean water, and strengthening residents' ability to remain in their homes amidst pressure from Israeli settlers and authorities. Muhannad al-Jaabari, the committee's director general, stated that "finding water means bringing life to the Old City," highlighting the project's role in addressing both practical water needs and the preservation of cultural heritage and residents' right to stay.

关注点 This event highlights the ongoing challenges faced by Palestinians in the occupied territories, specifically concerning access to basic resources like water, and the impact of Israeli occupation policies on their daily lives and heritage. The restoration of traditional wells represents a community-driven initiative to build resilience and preserve identity in the face of adversity.

Detailed English brief

Somalia ordered the Emirati ambassador to leave and recalled its own envoy from Abu Dhabi, marking a sharp break from a period of close security and commercial cooperation to open hostility. The breakdown stems from domestic power struggles within Somalia and the close ties of regional leaders with Abu Dhabi. Senior Emirati official Anwar Gargash called the decision "regrettable." This event is not just a diplomatic crisis between Somalia and the UAE but also reflects regional power plays and geopolitical complexities, potentially impacting Somalia's internal stability and its fight against al-Shabab.

关注点 The severing of ties between Somalia and the UAE highlights the complex geopolitical landscape of the Horn of Africa, where regional powers' influence and internal political dynamics are deeply intertwined. The dispute underscores the challenges of foreign partnerships in fragile states and the potential for external actors to exacerbate internal divisions, with significant implications for Somalia's stability and its ongoing fight against extremist groups.
