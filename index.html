美政府要求冰岛公司移除ICE员工数据，公司辩称公众利益

Trump administration attempts to take down leak of ICE personnel data
Al Jazeera · 2026-10-30 06:16
详细中文报道

美国国土安全部（DHS）已向一家冰岛网络托管公司FlokiNET发出要求，希望其移除一个包含数万名美国联邦执法人员信息的网站。周四，FlokiNET公布了其收到的来自网络安全公司ZeroFox代表DHS的一封信函。信中指控FlokiNET违反服务条款，发布了“被盗”的员工数据集，并要求其“移除或禁用”这些信息。 信函指出，“该内容在线发布，使超过4万名可识别个人面临骚扰、跟踪和人身伤害的风险。” FlokiNET表示，这封信函涉及的域名为icepi.cx，该网站汇集了包括移民及海关执法局（ICE）和海关及边境保护局（CBP）在内的多个DHS部门员工信息。自2025年特朗普总统的大规模驱逐行动开始以来，这些机构一直受到严格审查。 然而，特朗普政府经常以安全风险为由，拒绝透露参与具体行动的DHS特工身份。FlokiNET公司表示，尽管拒绝遵守DHS的要求，但该网站的注册商最终暂停了该域名。不过，员工信息随后已转移到另一个域名上。 FlokiNET在其在线声明中为不移除该网站的决定辩护，称：“联邦官员在执行执法项目时，并非私人。公众有正当理由了解谁在执行这项工作，在谁的授权下执行，以及在哪个办公室执行。” 该数据库最早于周三由Distributed Denial of Secrets (DDoSecrets)发布，这是一个专门展示被泄露政府数据的组织。此前，DDoSecrets曾发布过被定罪的性侵犯者杰弗里·爱泼斯坦的电子邮件，以及以色列司法部和美国警察部门的信息。 一个自称为“和平部”的黑客组织声称对获取和分享DHS数据负责。自数据库首次发布以来，其他组织已将信息传播到其他地方，包括一些网站。

Detailed English brief

The U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has requested that Icelandic web hosting company FlokiNET remove a website containing information on tens of thousands of U.S. federal law enforcement employees. FlokiNET disclosed receiving a letter from cybersecurity firm ZeroFox, acting on behalf of DHS, accusing the company of violating its terms of service by publishing a "stolen" employee dataset and demanding its removal. The letter stated that the data, hosted on the domain icepi.cx and compiled from agencies including Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) and Customs and Border Protection (CBP), puts over 40,000 individuals at risk. FlokiNET refused the request, arguing that federal officers carrying out enforcement programs are not private individuals and that the public has a legitimate interest in knowing who they are. The database was initially posted online by Distributed Denial of Secrets (DDoSecrets), a group known for releasing leaked government data. A hacker group calling itself the "Department of Peace" claimed responsibility for obtaining and sharing the DHS data. Although the website's registrar has suspended the domain, the employee information has reportedly moved to other platforms.

关注点 This incident highlights the ongoing tension between government efforts to protect the identities of law enforcement personnel, particularly those involved in sensitive operations like immigration enforcement, and the public's right to information and transparency. The involvement of a foreign-based hosting company and a data leak group complicates U.S. government efforts to control the dissemination of sensitive information. FlokiNET's defense, emphasizing public interest in knowing who is enforcing policies, reflects a broader debate about accountability and oversight of government agencies. The continued availability of the data on alternative platforms suggests that once leaked, such information is difficult to fully contain, posing persistent security and privacy risks.
