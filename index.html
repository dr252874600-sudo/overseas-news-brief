下一步观察： IMF的预测强调了全球经济面临的复杂性和不确定性。各国政府如何应对高通胀、高利率以及地缘政治冲突带来的挑战，将是影响未来经济走向的关键。特别是中东局势的演变，以及各国央行的货币政策走向，将是未来需要密切关注的焦点。

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has warned in its latest global economic outlook that persistent conflicts, high energy costs, and technological upheavals will slow global growth and raise prices. The Middle East war, in particular, is expected to widen the economic gap between rich and poor countries. The IMF has upgraded its growth forecast for the UK economy to 1.3% for 2026, an increase from its previous prediction of 1%.
